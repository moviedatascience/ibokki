## Analysis

SERIES: EXP-8 SHATTER CALIBRATION — m48, Abj (P0) vs Div (P1), seed 8501, bot greedy. RESULT: P0 (Abjuration) wins at round 12, HP 15 vs -10 — a decisive, non-close win, fully manual play throughout.

BANK TRAJECTORY: The prevention ledger ran exactly as rich as the brief predicted. Every blocked/absorbed doom fed it: Saboteur's Kit (2, zeroed twice by Stone Stance), Foreclosure (4, banked in full twice via exact-sized wards, partially other times), Far Sight (2, mostly wasted via oversized wards), Entropy (7, partial banks of 2/4 when ward was undersized). Two Reckoning casts converted the bank into finishing blows: 11 dmg (R10, 28->17) and 12 dmg (R11, 17->5), then a final Reckoning for 15 dmg closed the game (R12). Reckoning is non-consuming/repeatable and reads the LIFETIME total, so each successive cast (funded by continued absorption) dealt equal-or-more damage than the last — this is an extremely strong, possibly overtuned finisher once the bank is seeded.

SHATTER EVENTS AND BUMPER SIZING: multiple direct tests of the sizing minigame:
- 2HP ward vs Saboteur's Kit(2): perfect match, zero waste, banked full 2.
- 4HP ward vs Foreclosure(4) unreduced: perfect match, zero waste, banked full 4 (deliberately skipped Stone Stance that turn since reducing damage below the ward's size would still fully destroy the ward for LESS credit — a genuine sizing insight, not obvious from the card text alone).
- 6-8HP wards vs Far Sight(2)/Saboteur's(2): repeatedly wasteful — oversized wards fully destroyed for a mere 2 banked, confirming "bigger is not safer" against small dooms.
- 4HP ward vs Entropy(7) twice: ward fully consumed (banked its full 4), 3-5 overflow hit face directly since no second ward existed — correctly modeled, no double-shatter observed (no "next ward" to touch).
- Stone Stance (-2/round) reliably zeroed 2-dmg dooms outright when active, saving the ward entirely — the single best value play discovered.

SIZING MINIGAME VERDICT: real texture, not a tax — but only when the pilot tracks doom numbers and pre-sizes. It rewards knowing the opponent's revealed kit (Foreclosure=4, Far Sight/Saboteur=2, Entropy=7) and creates a genuine tension between "grow the ward now" vs "hold it small," and between "stack Stone Stance" vs "let the raw number land on an exact-sized bumper." A bot without this doom-book will systematically over- or under-size and leak value either way — this is a real skill gap the brief expected to find.

DOOM-FIRE REACTION BUG (significant finding): Echo Shield and Absorb, when armed via attached components, NEVER got offered as a reaction window when a "prophecy" doom actually fired (turn-start auto-resolve) — tested repeatedly across R5, R6, R8, R12 with reactions freshly armed mid-round (ruling out the round-boundary-sweep explanation). By contrast, instant-damage spells (Foretell) DID correctly prompt `react-abj-007` before resolving, and it fully negated the damage as expected. Passive effects (Stone Stance's flat -2) DID apply to dooms correctly. So the specific gap is: doom/prophecy arrivals bypass the reaction-window/stack machinery entirely and go straight to a hardcoded damage-application path — almost certainly a `SIMPLIFIED` auto-resolve stand-in per the project's known bug pattern (`grep -rn SIMPLIFIED packages/engine`). This makes Echo Shield/Absorb/Grounding/Dampen strictly useless against dooms in the current build, even though the brief's fiction ("your wards soak it... everything you absorb charges your bank") implies they should interact. Recommend checking the doom-arrival resolution path against the normal damage-reaction pipeline.

RUNIC SEAL DENIAL LOOP: repeatedly resealing Prophecy of Collapse (tracked via the original prep-order reveal, since P1 never re-revealed it explicitly) drove the bot to eventually abandon the card outright, swapping it for Calculated Draw around round 10 — direct evidence the seal-lock strategy is not just card-average-neutral but behaviorally correct (the bot recognized the dead card and cut it). Good validation that Runic Seal is a strong, low-cost (SS) permanent-denial tool against any single-target non-damage doom.
