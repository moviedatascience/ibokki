## Analysis

## Abjuration (piloted) vs Divination (greedy bot) — seed 2101

**RESULT: WIN for Abjuration, round 10. Final HP: Abj 21 / Div -9.** Kill shot: Ward Collapse [ABJ-031] sacrificing a 29-HP ward for 29 direct damage (their HP was 20). Bot-level baseline for this matchup was 0-30 Div (avg 10.2 rounds); the human-grade game won in exactly the doom-clock window the bot losses die in.

### What actually won the game
1. **Total doom denial.** Div's ONLY kill pressure in this deck was Omen (2 pierce/round) + Saboteur's Kit gambits (2 pierce, uncancellable). Over 10 rounds their total damage output on me was **9** (3× Omen ticks + 2× Saboteur's + 2 exhaustion... my HP path 30→21, with +5 Second Wind). Denials: Phase Shift cancelled Omen twice and Reclaim once; Runic Seal sealed the Omen slot twice pre-cast (once mid-assembly, stranding their M), sealed Reclaim once and Cut the Thread once. Every big Div round was reduced to economy spells + one 2-point doom at most.
2. **Ward stacking is secretly a wincon, not just defense.** The briefed plan (Reckoning/Final Reckoning) was miscalibrated: L3 doesn't unlock until level 10 (not 7) and L4 later still, AND the greedy bot **refuses to chip into wards** (its eval knows soak is worthless to it), so the Reckoning bank finished at ~6 prevented (=3 dmg). The real Abj wincon vs Div is **Ward Collapse**: 9 rounds of Fortify/+Ward Pulse/+Bulwark Shard built a 29-HP ward = a stored one-turn kill that pierces nothing (direct damage, and Div keeps no wards).
3. **Round-pacing control.** Rounds only end when a side exhausts cast slots. I deliberately exhausted my slots to (a) force round-end sweeps that stranded the bot's massive attached-M hoards (2 big fuel dumps swept), and (b) race levels toward L3. Conversely I stalled rounds when their deck was thinner to accelerate their exhaustion reshuffle (they took the first exhaustion-2; I took one too).
4. **Fuel-rescue micro.** Detaching unused reaction fuel to hand before round-end sweep (Phase Shift S,S saved twice) is a real skill edge the sim bots don't have (they never retract/rarely detach); it roughly doubled my effective component income in the mid-game.

### Tool scorecard
- **Phase Shift (L2, SS): the load-bearing card.** 3 cancels (Omen×2, Reclaim×1). Cheaper than Counterbind for an S-based school and cancels ANYTHING — strictly the better anti-doom tool for Abj. Never got to test Counterbind (never prepped).
- **Runic Seal (L1, SS): 4 casts, all useful** (Omen×2 including one mid-assembly strand, Reclaim×1, Cut-the-Thread×1). Seal is also the round-end pacing tool. Cancel > seal when both available (cancel burns their fuel), but seal works pre-cast and forces detach-shuffling.
- **Ward Pulse: quietly excellent.** 6 casts = 6 chip (their only HP loss until the kill besides 2 exhaustion) + ~20 total ward HP (scales +1/ward with 5-6 wards up). It kept the payload growing and the race honest.
- **Sentinel Rune (L2, VS): strong engine** — the draw-on-their-cast triggered most rounds (it dug out the kill-completing CMP-SS off their Foreknowledge cast, the literal winning draw).
- **Grounding: good early, dead mid** (2 uses R1-R2; the bot then stopped casting damage entirely). **Reckoning: never worth prepping this game** (bank ~6 because the bot won't feed it). **Quenching Salts (GAM-013): 100% dead** vs Div — drew 3 copies, tucked 2 with Arcane Study; Abj resource deck shouldn't run burn-hate vs M-schools.
- **Eaten dooms (correct economy): 3× Omen + 2× Saboteur's Kit = 9 HP.** Answers were spent only where they bought more than 2 HP of value.

### Engine notes / possible issues
- **2-component-card cap per spell** blocked SSS from being paid as S+S+S — is that intended for spells (vs the documented deck-construction 2-card cost cap)? It creates a real (interesting?) texture: SSS spells are gated on drawing multi-component cards; my kill waited ~3 turns on a CMP-SS draw.
- **Greedy bot never chips into wards** — correct greedy play per its eval, but it makes Div totally passive vs Abj: entire rounds of pass-pass while it hoards. It also **hoard-attaches and then lets round-end sweep eat the fuel** when the opponent controls round pacing — a bot blindspot worth a look (it lost 5+ attached components to sweeps I forced).
- **Saboteur's Kit (gambit doom) bypasses the stack** — no reaction window, uncancellable, unsealable. Fine at 2 dmg; would be alarming at higher numbers.
- Bot never used a Div reaction all game (does the Div archetype deck run any? Anticipate never appeared).
- Div's L3 preps (Echoes/Calculated Draw/Manipulate Fate) are all economy — vs a full-denial opponent the archetype has no L3 damage escalation before L4, which is exactly the window Abj kills in.

### Verdict
**Abj-vs-Div is clearly winnable at human level — and arguably favors Abjuration** with this line: deny the doom slot every round (Phase Shift ≫ Counterbind ≫ Seal), refuse to feed Cut the Thread, control round pacing, stack one giant ward, and cash it with Ward Collapse at L3 (level 10). The 30-0 bot result is a bot-valuation artifact, not a matchup truth: the sim Abj bot neither cancels dooms on schedule, rescues fuel from sweeps, nor understands Ward Collapse as a stored wincon (ward HP × 1 damage conversion). Load-bearing card: Phase Shift; hidden MVP: Ward Collapse + the Fortify line that feeds it.
