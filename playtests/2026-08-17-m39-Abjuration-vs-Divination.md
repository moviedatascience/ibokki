## Analysis

HYPOTHESIS: does a disciplined seal/cancel/battery line let Abjuration beat greedy-Div pre-fix? VERDICT: NO — the denial toolkit (Runic Seal, Phase Shift, Counterbind, Mana Drain) executed cleanly and repeatedly, but Abjuration had literally zero proactive damage output the entire game (Reckoning never surfaced as a prep option through lv11; bank stayed ~0-2 all game since only one non-pierce hit was ever blocked). Result: LOSS, round 11, HP0 (P0) vs HP24 (P1) — a lopsided margin proving this was structural starvation, not a close misplay.

KEY LINES THAT WORKED:
- Runic Seal denied Foreclosure (4dmg doom) three separate times (R6, R7, R9) = 12 damage avoided.
- Counterbind cancelled a Foretell cast outright (R6 T82) — zero damage, zero hand-peek.
- Phase Shift cancelled Unbind targeting my ward (R8 T128), saving it from destruction.
- Mana Drain fired 4/4 times it was loaded when the opponent attached a component — 100% reliability this game (contra the m23 report of it sometimes never firing).

WHERE IT BROKE:
- Foreclosure got through twice anyway (R5, R8) when I had no S components in hand to load Runic Seal that turn — pure resource-drought losses, not misplays.
- Entropy (L3, 7dmg doom) landed completely uncontested (R10) because I had zero loaded reactions that turn — this was the single biggest blow and triggered the death spiral (HP13->4 combined with a concurrent Saboteur's Kit).
- Final blow: Far Sight (2dmg doom) killed me at HP2 while I held two literally-useless Quenching Salts cards — no components, no gambits, nothing to do but pass.

LEDGER DATA:
- Bank never exceeded ~2 (one Foretell instance blocked by an Aegis-Charm ward, 7HP->5HP, banking 2 prevented damage).
- Warding Tithe was prepped R1-5 but its cast option never appeared castable (bank ~0) — I swapped it out R6 for Counterbind.
- Reckoning [ABJ-032] NEVER appeared as a preparable/swappable option despite the game reaching lv11 — worth flagging to design as a possible deck-construction gap for this preset, since the brief treats it as the central payoff.
- Zero ledger spenders (Warding Tithe/Sealed Verdict/Restoring Rune/Reckoning) fired even once this game.

UNCHARGED PIERCE-DOOM DAMAGE TOTAL (would feed the bank under the proposed half-rate fix): summing every "!" flagged doom hit — Sab Kit x4 (2 each=8), Omen x3 (2 each=6), Foreclosure x2 (4 each=8), Entropy x1 (7), Far Sight x1 (2) = 33 total pierce damage. Under a half-rate fix that's ~16-17 bank points across the game — enough to fund Warding Tithe (spend≤4) three-plus times and get Reckoning swinging for real damage, which this game never had access to at all. Plus ~4 more damage from two uncountered Foretell direct-damage instances (unclear if those were also pierce-flagged; they bypassed a not-yet-existing ward both times, so untested).

BOT BLIND SPOTS: the greedy Div bot played very passively for long stretches (many pure pass/pass turns, slow to commit) and never pushed for a fast kill — it won by pure attrition of an opponent that structurally could not answer back. This suggests Div doesn't even need to play well against this Abjuration configuration; it just needs to not actively help.
