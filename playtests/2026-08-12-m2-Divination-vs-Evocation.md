## Analysis

RESULT: WIN for Divination (P0), round 5. Final HP: Div 4, Evo -1. Seed 813, greedy Evo bot. Second consecutive piloted Div win at R5 with low HP (prior: seed 812, 2 HP) — the playbook reproduces.

DAMAGE LEDGER (31 total on a 30 HP opponent):
- Omen DIV-012: 4 casts, 4 dooms landed = 8. Recast every round R1-R4 without a miss.
- Foreclosure DIV-020: 1 cast = 4, the kill blow. Fired at the start of their FINAL turn (slots-exhausted turn) — doom ticks still run on that turn, which is exactly the checkmate geometry the playbook calls for.
- Saboteur's Kit GAM-019 x2: first landed 2; second was pure insurance (unfired). Both found by Div's own filtering (Foresight scry took Kit #1, Anticipate's draw delivered Kit #2).
- Foretell DIV-011: 5 casts (every round) = 10 + hand intel each time.
- Anticipate DIV-014: 5 fires (every round, never missed a refuel) = 5 + 5 draws.
- Battle Trance self-damage: 2 (they paid HP to boost Searing Word).
Scheduled/pierce damage = 14 of 31 (45%); chip (Foretell+Anticipate) = 15; their own gambit = 2.

DEFENSE: Foresight cast R4 + R5 (couldn't afford earlier; offense correctly prioritized). Measured saves: R4 Firebolt 3→2, Spark 2→1, Burning Hands 1→0 (floors at 0), R5 Searing Word 4→3 = ~4 HP saved. I ended at 4 HP — Foresight was exactly the survival margin. Premonition: 1 cast, drew 3 (multi-symbol bonus), funded the R3 double-doom-round; the engine slot earned its keep.

KEY DEVIATION FROM BRIEF: at L5 I swapped Foreclosure in over OMEN, not Premonition/Foresight. Same MM cost, double doom damage, and with ~5 M/round you never cast both dooms in one round anyway — keeping both the draw engine and the defense card while upgrading the doom slot dominated. Recommend this as the default L5 line when fuel is the constraint.

EVO BRICK ASSESSMENT: Moderate and late, but decisive for the margin. R1-R4 the bot used every cast slot (4-6 dmg/round, textbook). R5 collapse: passed T23 entirely (hand 1, no V), and — the big one — Inferno Lance and Hex Bolt were prepped at L5 but NEVER CAST (V drought all round; they fell back on Burning Hands + Searing Word + Battle Trance). The advertised 6-8 spike never materialized. Without the brick I still likely win the race (their HP was 3 with doom 4 queued regardless), but I'd have been at ~1 HP or dead if both L5 spells had fired — the WIN was skill/geometry, the 4 HP MARGIN was seed luck.

NEW MECHANICAL LEARNINGS (not in brief):
1. Burn markers tick at the END of the opponent's turns and DECAY (observed: burns for 1, later 2, later 1, then gone — total 4 damage from 3 Burning Hands). Not a permanent 1/round as briefed; absorbing burns rather than prepping Divine was correct.
2. Battle Trance costs Evo 2 HP (self-damage for a damage boost) — in a doom race the bot paying 2 HP accelerated its own death; boosted Searing Word to 4 (3 after Foresight).
3. Searing Word discards exactly one random hand COMPONENT — the attach-everything-you'll-use-this-round discipline blanked most of its value (it only got a spare CMP-M).
4. Doom arrival on the slots-exhausted final turn is confirmed lethal-before-action: 'X gets one final turn' does not skip doom ticks.
5. Anticipate did 1 dmg through Foresight-like symmetry every single round and its draw twice delivered exactly the needed card (Kit #2, spare M) — flat but high-reliability value; never skip the refuel.
6. Bot showed zero reaction usage all game (no pre-attached reaction components ever seen in their prep) — casting into their board was always safe this matchup.

READ: Div's game plan (dooms + chip + one defensive shave) is real and pilot-executable at ~5-6 damage/round when M flows. Two data points now say Div beats greedy Evo around R5 by ~2-4 HP — winnable but razor-thin, heavily dependent on Evo's V-fuel variance in the L5 spike round. The 0-240 sim result remains a bot-valuation gap (sim bots don't run this doom-scheduling/checkmate line), not a card-power gap.
