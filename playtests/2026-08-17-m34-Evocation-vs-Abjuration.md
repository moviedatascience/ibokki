## Analysis

Result: LOSS for Evocation (P0), R10, 0-32 HP. Decisive blow was a single Reckoning cast for 16 damage at R10 open (their bank had accumulated far more "prevented damage" credit than expected), followed by a 4-damage Ward Collapse finisher next turn.

Hypothesis verdict: bank-starving got HARDER, not easier, under the ledger family — but not for the reason expected. The spenders (Tithe/Rune) didn't need MY chip damage to fund themselves; the wards they create from spent bank then get hit BY my own attacks, and every point those wards absorb re-feeds the bank (soak counts as prevented). This created a feedback loop: Tithe spends bank -> ward -> I attack the ward (forced to, since my deck is V-heavy with no reliable non-feeding removal) -> ward absorbs -> bank re-fed -> more Tithe next round. I only saw ONE Dispelling Powder all game (R1) and no second one — with only one gambit copy seen, I had no repeatable way to starve the bank; I was structurally forced to feed it via ordinary combat.

Critical corrected finding: wards do NOT reset at round boundary in this ruleset (I initially misread several early transitions and wasted the entirety of R5 hoarding under that false assumption — a costly self-inflicted error, corrected by R6). Wards persist round to round until spent/destroyed; only spell/resource ATTACHMENTS sweep at round end. This is the single most important correction for future Evo pilots on this matchup — do NOT hold a full turn/round waiting for a wall to expire for free.

Second key finding: overkill damage from a single spell DOES cascade through multiple ward stacks in the array (confirmed: Fireball 5 dmg into a [3,3,...] wall left [1,...], i.e. 3 destroyed + 2 carried into the next stack) — so big single hits are efficient once the wall is thin enough, but a many-small-stack wall still eats casts one stack at a time when the hit is smaller than the lead stack.

Absorb (ABJ-011) is the single scariest interaction found: it reads the FULL PRE-mitigation spell damage, so casting Detonate (18 dmg, built off a hoarded 9-card hand) directly into an armed Absorb let them heal 9 and net them the whole round — worse than doing nothing. Two prior "bait" casts (Firebolt 3, Lightning Bolt 4) failed to draw it out; the greedy bot correctly held Absorb for the biggest threat on the stack. Hex Bolt (EVO-012, "cannot be the target of Reactions") is the correct tech answer — it flatly can't be Absorbed — but at only 3 base damage it's too small alone to matter; Evocation doesn't have a bigger Reaction-immune hitter available at the levels reached this game.

Ledger telemetry observed: Warding Tithe cast at least 5 times (R3, R4, R6, R7, R9 — each spending up to 4 bank into a fresh ward), Restoring Rune cast once (R8, healed 3, spent from bank), Reckoning cast once at R10 open for 16 damage (implying a lifetime-prevented bank of ~31-32 at that point — consistent with the 5 Tithe casts plus several ward absorbs feeding it all game). No Sealed Verdict [ABJ-047] was seen. The bank clearly stayed well-fed throughout because nearly every one of my casts this game landed into an active ward (I drew only one true bank-avoiding tool, Dispelling Powder, in the whole 10-round game).
