## Analysis

LOSS — P0 (Abjuration) dies to a locked-in Foreclosure doom at round 11 turn-start, HP -2 vs P1's HP 28. Hypothesis from m31-m33 HOLDS: the ledger family is starved by pierce/prophecy damage in this matchup, even with disciplined seal/cancel play and round-pacing denial.

KEY FINDINGS:

1. LEDGER STARVATION CONFIRMED AND QUANTIFIED. The damage-prevented bank only ever charged from Absorb blocking actual immediate-damage spells (Foretell, 2 dmg twice = 4 total banked across the match). Every prophecy-type doom (Foreclosure x4 attempts, Omen x2, Entropy x1, Far Sight x1, Saboteur's Kit x3) that connected dealt its full damage with ZERO credit to the bank — confirmed directly: casting Warding Tithe was rejected as illegal ("no legal action") whenever bank was 0, and only became castable right after an Absorb-blocked Foretell. Ledger spenders fired exactly twice this whole game (Warding Tithe, 2 HP ward and then 2 HP ward again) — both funded by the same 2-damage Absorb block being recharged. Reckoning/Sealed Verdict/Restoring Rune never had a viable bank (Sealed Verdict needs 6, bank peaked at 2).

2. UNCHARGED-DOOM-DAMAGE TOTAL (would have charged the ledger under a half-rate pierce-feeds-ledger fix): Saboteur's Kit 2+2+2=6, Omen 2+2=4, Foreclosure 4+4+4+4=16 (one more cancelled, one more locked-in-fatal), Entropy 7, Far Sight cancelled (0). Landed uncharged doom damage = 6+4+16+7 = 33 points. At a hypothetical 50% ledger rate that's ~16-17 additional bank capacity across the match — enough for one Sealed Verdict cancel (6) plus several Warding Tithe/Restoring Rune activations. This is the single most useful number for the pending design decision.

3. CANCEL-TYPE REACTIONS (Phase Shift, Counterbind) are the ONLY real answer to prophecy dooms — they stop the spell before the prophecy inscribes. Both landed clean cancels on live Foreclosure/Far Sight casts (3 total cancels across the game: 1x Counterbind on Foreclosure, 2x Phase Shift on Foreclosure/Far Sight). CONFIRMED BLIND SPOT: Absorb ("prevent all damage from one spell that targets you") does NOT stop prophecy inscription — reacting to a Foreclosure/Omen cast with Absorb wastes the reaction; the prophecy still inscribes and fires in full later. This was tested explicitly twice early in the match. Old-deck doctrine's "Absorb answers direct damage" is correct; "Absorb answers dooms" is false and should be corrected in doctrine notes.

4. ROUND-END PACING (exhausting own slots to force early round-end) worked exactly as designed — 4 separate rounds (R1, R6 turn82, R8, R9) were forced to end early via slot exhaustion, sweeping the opponent's unfunded/half-funded MM investments (including a fully-funded but uncast Omen once) for free. This is a real, repeatable, bot-exploitable line: Div-greedy bot stalls with hoarded MM attachments far more often than it should, and slot-exhaustion punishes that for zero cost.

5. FATAL SEQUENCE: round 10 saw Entropy (L3, 7 dmg/3t, MMM) enter the bot's book and get cast on the SAME turn it was revealed, with zero funded reaction available to answer it (I had only just finished sealing a different slot that turn, spending my Seal). This is the critical lesson: once L3 dooms are live (round 10+), the bot can one-shot-fund and cast a 7-damage prophecy in a single turn from a cold slot — holding Runic Seal for "target identification" is too slow against this; must seal proactively/blind on any newly-appeared unknown slot the instant it's prepped, every round, not just fund reactively.

6. Even with 3 clean cancels, HP still crossed from 30 to 2 by round 10 purely from the doom damage that DID land (Entropy 7, one uncancelled Foreclosure 4, two Omens 4, three Saboteur's Kit gambit-dooms 6 — gambit dooms are explicitly uncancellable/unsealable per brief and bypass all denial). The two exhaustion reshuffles (2 dmg) also chipped in. Total unavoidable chip damage over 10 rounds vastly outpaces the ~2 HP of ward value the ledger ever produced.

7. Mana Drain: armed once in round 1, fired exactly once (bounced an opponent MM attach) before its prep slot was auto-swapped out by an autoplay excursion in round 2-4 (see caveat below). Consistent with the ~21/30-game firing rate noted in doctrine; no anomaly observed.

CAVEAT: An early `autoplay(until: reactionWindow)` call (intended only to skip mechanical passes) ran much further than expected — 60 decisions spanning rounds 2-5 — and let the bot pilot make real prep-swap decisions on my behalf (replacing Runic Seal/Mana Drain with Absorb/Reflective Ward), plus several turns of attach/cast choices. This means rounds 2-5 are NOT purely hand-piloted; treat that stretch as bot-quality play, with hand-piloted decisions resuming from round 5's Absorb-vs-Foreclosure reaction onward. The final, most decision-dense rounds (6-11) are fully hand-piloted.

VERDICT: Hypothesis HOLDS at m37. Even a disciplined seal/cancel/pacing line — 3 clean spell cancels, 4 forced early round-ends denying opponent investment, careful reaction-holding — could not overcome the structural mismatch: cancels only answer spells while still on the stack (a narrow window, and only ~1 per round is realistically fundable+timed correctly), while prophecy/gambit dooms bypass wards, reduction, AND the ledger entirely. Div's doom density (2-3 threats live per round by round 6+) exceeds Abj's realistic answer rate (~1/round), and the ledger contributes essentially nothing to close the gap. Bot-level 7% looks like a fair upper bound for bot-vs-bot, but skilled piloting did not find a winning line pre-fix in this game either — 0/1 (piloted), consistent with 0/3 at m31-m33.
