## Analysis

**Result:** P1 (Evocation) wins on HP, round 5. Final HP: P0 (Divination) -1, P1 (Evocation) 7. Evocation ground me down from 30 to dead over 5 rounds while I brought them from 30 to 7 — a 23-point swing in my favor that still wasn't enough because their damage arrived in bigger, faster bursts (Firebolt 3, Inferno Lance 4, Searing Word 1-4 w/ Battle Trance, Hex Bolt for the kill) while mine was metronomic chip (Foretell 2/round, Omen 2/round, Anticipate 1/react) plus two slow-fused prophecies (Foreclosure 4, Omen 2x) that never got to land in bulk before I ran out of HP.

**Does the doom-clock line survive the new decks?** Partially, with one big correction.
- Omen R1-T1 every round: CONFIRMED easy. A single CMP-MM dual funded it outright in R1/R2/R4 with zero waste — exactly the "one MM card pays it" prediction. Recast R1, R2, R4 (skipped R3 on a genuine resource-drought turn — hand was gambit-only).
- Foreclosure timed for the kill: DID NOT SURVIVE AS BRIEFED, for a different reason than expected — it isn't a spellbook-composition problem, it's a *leveling-gate* problem. DIV-020 wasn't a legal prep option until R5 (char level 5, "Max spell level" gate lifted from L1 to L2). Before that the prep-phase legal-action list literally didn't offer it. Once unlocked I swapped Premonition out for it and fired it immediately (R5, 7 HP target), but by then I was at 1 HP with only one more round in me — it never got to be the "exact kill" tool, just a last hail-mary that landed for prophecy value after game-relevant time had passed.
- Foretell every round: CONFIRMED, cast every round I had a turn (R1,R2,R3,R4,R5) — 2 dmg + intel each time, reliable value.
- Anticipate armed every window: PARTIALLY. Doctrine's seat-based timing rule replicated exactly — could NOT fire on opponent's first cast in rounds where they had first seat (R1, R3), COULD and DID fire on their first cast in rounds where I had first seat (R4). Repeatedly caught flat unfunded on their follow-up casts (R2 Firebolt, R5 Inferno Lance) because a reaction can only be funded during a prior main-phase window, and I only had 1-2 spare component cards most turns — funding Anticipate competed directly with funding Omen/Foretell for the same scarce M pips. This felt like the single biggest lever I couldn't pull hard enough: with richer M-duals this game I *could* afford to prefund it more often than I did, but the offense-vs-defense allocation was genuinely tight at 2-3 slots/round.
- Premonition first on 3-slot rounds: NOT REALLY TESTED — swapped it out for Foreclosure once Foreclosure unlocked (R5), and before that never found the components to spare (M pips went to Omen/Foretell). No Saboteur's Kit found via Premonition, but one was drawn naturally off the top deck (R3) and played for free stack-bypass prophecy value, matching the doctrine's description of the card even though the discovery path (draw, not dig) differed.
- Foresight underrated defense: STRONGLY CONFIRMED, and arguably the game's real headline. Cast at 6 HP (R4) and again at 1 HP (R5). The second cast's -1/spell reduction turned a would-be-lethal Searing Word (1 dmg, template) into 0 damage and kept me alive one extra full round. Without that single card I lose by R4T20, not R5T30.
- Near-zero wasted M: mostly held. Two genuine misattach errors this game (attaching a funding component to an already-cast prep slot instead of the intended one) were both caught and corrected via detach same-turn, so no component was actually burned — but it cost extra tool-turns and is worth flagging as an easy UI trap (attach-slug indices don't obviously map to slot position at a glance).
- Carried burn marker: seen once (Burning Hands R3->R4, ticked for 1 at my R4 turn start) but was NOT the kill this time — unlike the m16 pilot reference, I survived past it. Notably it did NOT reappear after — opponent never landed a second Burning Hands.

**Evocation clock speed vs old references:** Old piloted wins (m1-m4, m16-m18) went ~4-0 / 2-1 in Divination's favor riding Evo bricks; bot-level baseline for this edge is "100% Evo, ~5.6 rounds." This game: Evocation won in 5 rounds almost exactly on the bot-level clock-speed reference, and I did NOT see the brick behavior that padded the old piloted wins — opponent's casts were dense and rarely wasted (only two full "pass without casting" turns the entire game: R3 T11-ish gaps and one R5 turn). The new VV duals (8 in their deck per the brief) showed up as advertised: Inferno Lance (VV, 4 dmg) landed R5 as a genuine nuke I had no answer for, and Hex Bolt (VV, cost) delivered the kill. This reads as real evidence the deck redesign closed the brick gap that used to save Divination bot-side.

**Anomalies:**
1. Battle Trance (neutral gambit, "lose 2 HP, next spell +3 dmg") turned R1's Searing Word from 1 dmg into 4 — a big self-buff swing from a card that costs the caster 2 HP; worth checking bot valuation of trainer gambits that trade own-HP for burst, since the bot used it turn 1 confidently.
