## Analysis

RESULT: P0 (Divination) wins on HP, round 6, final HP 6-(-1). Margin: not razor-thin — Div built a commanding lead by round 5 (opp 8hp vs my 13hp) and coasted to victory.

Doom share of total damage: Div dealt roughly 24-31 total damage to Evo (30 starting hp -> -1). Doom-class effects (Omen 2x, Saboteur's Kit 2x, Foreclosure 1x) totaled 4+4+4=12 damage — a large plurality of total damage dealt, consistent with prior series findings that dooms are the backbone of Div's offense.

Foresight uptime: cast every single round (R1-R5), 5/5 rounds — full uptime. This mattered: Firebolt was measurably reduced (3dmg instead of 4 observed R3), and it also cycled cards each cast.

Anticipate uptime: funded and fired on essentially every opponent spell cast once attached (funding lagged by ~1 turn each round since it competed for early-turn mana with Foresight/Omen funding) — landed 4 reactive hits across the game for 4 chip damage plus card draws.

DECISIVE LINES:
1. R3-R4: discovering GAM-019 Saboteur's Kit (a Gambit "Prophecy" duplicate of Omen) drawn via Foresight's dig — played it as a FREE doom clock that bypasses the one-spell-per-turn limit entirely. This let me double up doom clocks (Omen + Saboteur's Kit same turn) for 4 damage on a single turn's mana, which is exactly the kind of resource-efficiency edge the brief was fishing for.
2. R5 prep: swapped Omen (MM, 2dmg doom) for Foreclosure (MM, 4dmg doom) once L2 spells unlocked — same cost, double damage. This single swap turned a 7hp opponent into a dead one two turns later without needing to draw into anything.
3. R4 T20: played Disarm (gambit) to strip opponent's last hand component on their forced final turn, denying any possible defensive play — pure tempo denial that cost me nothing.
4. Never needed Cut the Thread/hand disruption against Mana Burn — opponent's Mana Burn (a real threat, cancels M-cost spells + 2dmg) never got funded in time to matter; Foresight's digging kept me ahead of the clock enough that opponent's mana was always behind.

SURVIVAL-TOOL-NEEDED MOMENTS: none this game — HP never dropped below 6, and the closest real danger was R5 T23 Wrath of the Mage (4dmg) which Foresight reduced to 3. No round required a defensive tool that doesn't exist; this game the race was won going away rather than razor-thin.

HYPOTHESIS VERDICT: Does NOT replicate the 1-2 razor-race on this seed. Bot-Evo bricked its funding of Mana Burn (a real answer to Div's M-heavy spellbook) and never assembled the R5 Wrath-of-Mage+Mana Burn combo that could have punished my open casts. The decisive skill differential was NOT Foresight uptime alone (both had full uptime by design) but (a) drafting Foreclosure over Omen at the moment L2 unlocked for double doom value at equal cost, and (b) recognizing Saboteur's Kit as a free stacking doom clock via Gambit's stack-bypass — a line the bot's heuristic/greedy valuation likely undervalues since gambits are off-stack and cheap to overlook in a rollout horizon. This suggests the earlier bot-vs-bot 0% Div rate is at least partly a PILOTING gap (bots not drafting/exploiting Foreclosure-for-Omen swaps or doom-stacking via gambits), not purely a structural Div deficiency — consistent with the PILOT-GAP DOCTRINE. Recommend more piloted reps before concluding Div needs a round-5 survival tool; this game never needed one.

BOT BLIND SPOTS: bot-Evo never funded Mana Burn (VM) despite holding relevant components across multiple turns near round 5-6, missing a real answer to my M-heavy doom spells. Bot-Evo's Battle Trance self-damage (2dmg self, R1) accelerated its own clock for no observed benefit that turn (opponent had no funded spell to combo it with immediately) — matches brief's note that Evo's self-damage is a liability in a doom race.
