## Analysis

PILOT-vs-PILOT game 2 (series: Div 2-0 after this game). Both seat reports,
condensed; full versions in the session log.

### Seat 0 — Divination pilot (winner, R13, 20 HP vs -3)

Bank starving fully validated: zero-credit Unbind/Prophecy of Collapse
destroys plus Counter-Plan cancels of Stone Stance kept the ledger thin —
Reckoning cashed exactly ONCE (7, R11). Prophecy of Collapse pre-cast before
wards even existed erased every Tithe/item ward for free, including a 7-HP
three-item investment. Foreclosure/Entropy beating the -2 stance floor by raw
numbers was the actual kill engine once L2/L3 unlocked; the final sequence was
Unbind the last ward, Counter-Plan the emergency Stone Stance, Entropy for an
unmitigated lethal 7. Discovered: paying a cost with 2 separate cards dodges
Aegis's single-component clause; own-sequencing is Counter-Plan's real limiter
(front-load its components EVERY round — they act first on alternating
rounds). Fortress cleanly no-sold an Unbind (the exp-8d fix working live).
Sealed Vault reset the deck 7→32, deleting the exhaustion clock (balance
flag). Their Phase Shift/Counterbind answered Foreclosure and Unbind once
each — the cancel duel cuts both ways.

### Seat 1 — Abjuration pilot (loser)

Counter-Plan sniped 6 casts (Stone Stance x3, Reflective Ward x2 + more) and
CORRECTED the playbook: overfunding with one dual/triple card does NOT
protect — the strip removes the physical card, zeroing however many pips it
carried. Only 2+ SEPARATE cards protect a cost. The Fortress save at R12 (4-HP
protected ward under a live Entropy; Unbind bounced; the ward then soaked 4 of
the reduced 5) was the tactical highlight and bought a full extra round.
Reckoning arrived at the L3 unlock (~round 10 on the ramp) and cashed once for
7 — the whole proactive output. Fatal shape: the round-end sweep of ALL
attached components (reactions included) forces refunding defenses from zero
every round, and their dooms were timed into exactly those windows.
Structural flag for design review: the sweep is a severe tempo tax on the
reactive school vs a doom-scheduling opponent; alternatively Counter-Plan's
power deserves a look.

### Orchestrator synthesis

The most one-sided game of the series, and the mechanism is now legible:
whoever wins the cancel duel (Counter-Plan vs Stone Stance/Reckoning, with
Counterbind as the counter-counter) controls both economies. Abjuration's
tools all worked when they fired — Fortress, bumpers, the one Reckoning — but
sustained cancel pressure plus zero-credit removal kept the engine below
stall speed. Design-flag cluster to carry: reaction-funding sweep, Counter-Plan
rate, Sealed Vault reset.
