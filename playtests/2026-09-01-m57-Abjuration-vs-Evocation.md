## Analysis

Result: Abjuration (P0) wins by Overcharge one-shot, round 10, HP 28-0.

Decisive lines:
- Stone Stance re-cast every round (with the new +1 buffs, Fireball 6->4, Inferno Lance 5->3, Searing Word/Hex Bolt reduced to 0-1) meant nearly every Evo spell this game either did 0-1 direct damage or fed the ward.
- Fortify/Ward Pulse recast loop grew a single ward to 11 HP by round 10, soaking Inferno Lance (twice) and Firebolt fully multiple times with damage banked into the prevention ledger.
- Warding Tithe (R6) converted 4 banked prevention into a fresh ward for free tempo.
- Absorb was armed (SS) as insurance from R5 on but never needed to fire — the ward always ate the hit first, so Absorb sat as a banked answer the whole game (never triggered — a genuine "wasted arm" cost, though cheap insurance).
- Overcharge (prepped R10) was the kill: armed SSV over two turns (VSM + SS components), timed to fire exactly when ward HP*2 >= opponent HP (ward 9 -> 18 dmg vs opponent's 18 HP) for a precise lethal one-shot. Opponent's own deck-exhaustion reshuffle (self-inflicted 2 dmg) helped set up the exact number.

HYPOTHESIS VERDICT: Piloted Abj found its edge comfortably against buffed greedy-Evo — never dropped below 23/30 HP, ward never ran out, and closed with a clean precision kill rather than a grind. The +1 bumps on Fireball/Lance did NOT break the ward-sizing plan; recasting Stone Stance every round shaves them right back down (Fireball 6->4, Lance 5->3), so a 4-8 HP ward still ate them whole exactly as before the buffs. Bots' greedy-Evo continued casting into wards and building hand size (deck hit 0 once) without ever landing a haymaker — no Detonate/Sunburst/Meteor appeared this game, and Mana Burn (prepped R6) was never cast since none of my spells cost M this game (Overcharge is SSV, not M-inclusive) so it never got the chance to punish it.

Ward-sizing math with new numbers: under Stone Stance, Fireball=4, Lance=3, Hex Bolt=1, Searing Word=0, Wrath of the Mage=2, Cataclysm(unseen)=2. A ward of 4+ HP fully eats any single non-buffed Evo spell under Stone Stance; only gambit-boosted casts (Battle Trance +3, Empowered Chalk +1) punched through a 4HP ward for overflow damage (happened twice, R3 and R5, costing me 2+1=3 direct HP total across the game — trivial).

Stoke/Mana Burn: neither meaningfully appeared. Mana Burn was prepped by the bot from R6 on but I never gave it an M-cost target to snipe (Absorb/Overcharge use S/V not M), so the buffed Mana Burn's new reaction-cancel clause never got tested against my Counterbind-equivalent — a gap worth another pilot game explicitly casting M-cost Abj spells to see if the bot actually fires Mana Burn on them.

Bot blind spots: (1) greedy-Evo kept casting chip spells into a growing ward instead of holding for a burst turn — it never recognized the ward was strictly negating its damage output round over round. (2) It hoarded hand size (5 cards, deck hit 0) apparently building toward Detonate but never cast it before I closed the game — either it doesn't have Detonate in this decklist or its threshold logic never triggered.
