PILOTED CALIBRATION, NEW-DECK (game 1 of 3): Abjuration (Claude, P0) vs greedy Evocation bot, seed 6101, new resource decks (15 primary basics / 8 same-symbol duals / cross-duals 2 VS + 6 SM for Abj, 6 VM for Evo). The new-deck edge is a wash to very slightly slower on the clock (11 rounds vs 10) but with a HEALTHIER final margin (32 HP vs 29 HP) — no sign of a faster or scarier Evo clock at skilled Abj play. If anything the greedy bot was passive: it took entire half-rounds (R7 back half, all of R8) doing nothing but attaching-and-holding despite funded slots, and never found a proactive line against the wall.

FACE DAMAGE TAKEN: only 4 HP total across 11 rounds — 1 from Firebolt (R2 T7, before that round's Stone Stance was up) and 3 from Hex Bolt (R6 T29-30, immediately after Dispelling Powder had just wiped my Ward and before Stone Stance was cast that round). Every other point of incoming damage across ~9 opposing spell casts (2x Spark, 3x Firebolt, 3x Burning Hands+burn, Wrath of the Mage x2, Hex Bolt, Searing Word, Chain Lightning, Fireball x2) was fully absorbed by Stone Stance's -2 blanket and/or the Ward. Net HP swing was positive: -4 from damage, +6 from gambits (Quenching Salts +1, Second Wind +5), finishing at 32 vs the 30 start.

STONE STANCE EXCHANGE RATE: cast every single round, 10/10 (R1–R10), never skipped — a cleaner record than the old-deck reference (m8 skipped once for a Stonewarden swap). Funding was never a bottleneck: an exact-fit S component was available or spare S/SM/VSM symbols could always cover it. New-deck engine observation: Stone Stance's -2 reduction was confirmed to also blank Burn-marker ticks within the same round it's active — a 1-damage Burning Hands burn tick fully disappeared (no Ward or HP change) while Stone Stance was up (R5 T25), not just the direct cast damage. This roughly doubles its practical value against Evo's burn subtheme and should be noted if it's not the intended reading.

RECKONING LEDGER: one cast, R10 T63, for 19 damage (opponent 20→1), off a lifetime-prevented bank of ~37-38 (ceil(bank/2)=19). Feed sources: 10 Fortify casts (+2 Ward HP each = 20), 2x Bulwark Shard (+2 each = 4), 1x Aegis Charm (+3) = 27 raw Ward HP generated over the game, plus the Stone Stance blanket's own direct-damage prevention on every uncountered cast. The new 8-SS-dual resource shape meant Reckoning's own SS cost was trivial to fund the instant L3 unlocked (level 10, as documented) — cast the same turn it was prepared, no waiting round for fuel. One cast was lethal-adjacent by itself; the game closed out one round later on a single Ward Pulse ping.

NEW-DECK SHAPE EFFECTS: never resource-starved on the Abj side — every round had spare symbols after funding the full ward-stack (Stone Stance + Fortify + Ward Pulse, sometimes Echo Shield too), consistent with "multi-symbol assembly much easier." The rare VS dual (2 copies in the 40-card deck) showed up exactly twice and both times covered an S-cost spell with the V wasted — never a bottleneck since SM/SS covered everything. On the Evo side, VVV-cost L3 spells (Meteor, Chain Lightning, Pyroclasm) were preparable and two of three were actually cast (Chain Lightning, and Fireball at VV) — burst assembly was clearly faster/easier than pre-redesign, but it didn't matter: Stone Stance + a fat Ward absorbed a 7-tier hit as easily as a 2-tier one, so raw burst speed upgrades don't move this matchup unless they come with evasion/negation of the wall itself.

ANOMALIES / BOT BLINDSPOTS: (1) Dispelling Powder (neutral gambit, either side can run it) one-shot my Ward for free (R6) — same finding as the old-deck reference; ward-destroy is still the only real lever the bot has and it used it exactly once. (2) The greedy bot spent R7's second half and the entirety of R8 passing every turn despite having funded slots (VV-shaped, possibly Combust) sitting ready — no cast, no reaction, nothing; hand grew to 6 while its deck (40 cards) ran down to empty by R9, triggering a deck-exhaustion reshuffle that cost it 2 self-inflicted damage (R10) — a notable anti-hoarding rule that punished the bot's own passivity. (3) Mana Burn (Evo's M-cancel reaction) was dead the entire game: none of my prepared spells required an M component in their cost shape (all S/SS/VS), so it never had a legal target despite being prepared for six rounds — worth flagging as a card that's currently uncastable against the doctrine loadout. (4) Combust's presence/identity was never confirmed (opponent kept several slots face-down/funded without casting), so the new reaction-legality enforcement (Combust only answers my reactions) was never directly stress-tested this game — I stayed disciplined and simply never reacted while a suspicious VV slot sat funded, per doctrine, and it cost nothing since Absorb/Echo Shield/Shatter Ward were never actually needed to survive.

PILOT PROCESS NOTE: three misattach corrections this game (funding the wrong prepared-spell slot after the legal-action index list shifted between attach calls) — each was caught and fixed same-turn via detach+reattach at zero net cost, but it's a recurring pilot-tool friction (slugs like `att-cmp-ss-1` are reused across multiple same-shaped cards in hand, and indices shift after each action) worth a note for future sessions: re-read the fresh legal list before reusing a remembered index rather than assuming slot-order stability.
