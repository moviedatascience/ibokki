RECKONING BANK PROGRESSION: ~2 (R1) -> ~6 (R2) -> ~10 (R3) -> ~13 (R4) -> ~20 (R5) -> ~25 (R7) -> ~30 (R8) -> ~35 (R9) -> 40 by mid-R10. One cast at L10 dealt 20 (23->3). WARD ECONOMY VS CHIP: their whole-game output into my wards/stance never outpaced Fortify+Pulse+Shard income; wards peaked at 17 total HP and ended at 19. Burn ticks routed into wards (bank, not HP). Dispelling Powder (R1) killing a 3HP ward uncompensated was their best single play of the game — ward-destroy effects are the real Abj counterplay, and the bot only had one.

THEIR REACTIONS' IMPACT: zero. Backdraft/Riposte/Volatile Bolt/Combust never fired once. I timed casts for unfueled windows (their fuel state is public), never reacted while Combust was armed, and used a VS instead of an SM card once specifically to dodge Volatile Bolt. Their armed reactions then rotted: the bot hoarded armed fuel into the round-end attachment sweep TWICE (R7: 6 fuel cards swept including armed Inferno Lance and Combust; R8: 3 more) — that is the detach-before-round-end blindspot on the Evo side and it cost the bot roughly a third of its total fuel income.

BOT PUNISHES/SURPRISES: (1) Searing Word appeared at L3 (it is L1, not L2 as my brief assumed) and sniped a banked CMP-SS out of hand — my only discipline lapse; after that I held only gambits and it whiffed twice, so the bot kept wasting V on it. (2) Dispelling Powder R1. (3) Round-seating alternates, so on even seats it lands one pre-stance hit per round — Inferno R9 (3+2burn) all soaked. (4) After Stonewarden landed (R6), the bot's L1 spells could not target me and its tempo collapsed into multi-turn passes; it never found the line of chewing the 2HP Stonewarden ward with L2s to re-enable the cantrips (it kept growing anyway via Pulse).

VERDICT: at skilled play this edge is not 53-47, it is overwhelming Abj — I'd estimate 90%+ vs greedy Evo. The triangle intent (Abj > Evo) is realized mechanically; the bot-vs-bot number understates it because greedy Evo cannot price (a) stance-timing around public fuel state, (b) never feeding armed-fuel to the sweep, (c) the compounding double-count of ward soak into Reckoning. Piloted series on this pairing now 1-0.

MISPRICED FEELINGS: Stone Stance at S is brutally efficient vs a cantrip school — 1 card negates ~4-5 damage/round AND banks it; consider Riposte-class punishes actually firing more often as the counterweight. Stonewarden (SS) is arguably format-warping into Evo: persistent L1-cast lockout on a ward that other cards grow; the counter (chip the specific ward) needs the bot to value re-enabling its own cantrips. Reckoning double-counting ward soaks means vs spam decks it reliably one-shots for 15-20 by L10; if that is not intended, count only stance/reaction prevention, or halve ward-soak credit. Evo deck exhaustion (their 40-card deck burned fast on Recharge/Arcane Study digging) delivering lethal 2 exhaustion damage is a fitting anti-hoard rule that ended it cleanly.
