## Analysis

SERIES: LEDGER-ERA TRIANGLE, leg Evocation-vs-Abjuration, m35 of m34-m36.

RESULT: P1 (Abjuration, greedy bot) wins by Reckoning damage, round 14. Final HP: P0 -2, P1 7.

SUMMARY: Evocation dominated the damage race for most of the game — opponent was ground down from 30 to as low as 5-6 HP multiple times (R6, R11, R13) via consistent Fireball/Kindle/Crackle pressure plus one big Meteor. But Evocation's own HP bled out FASTER via repeated Reckoning casts (6, 6, 6, 4 damage across rounds 10-14) stacked on top of Battle Trance/Dispelling-adjacent self-damage and reflected-ward chip. The kill-before-R9 doctrine flatly failed to execute: the game dragged to R14 because (a) Abjuration's healing (Restoring Rune, Second Wind) kept resetting the opponent from single digits back to 8-17 HP every couple rounds, and (b) an early Absorb ate a fully-hoarded Detonate (12 potential damage + Searing Riposte unarmed) for a 10->16 HP swing at R6 — the single most decisive misplay of the game.

DECISIVE LINES:
- R6 T46: Cast Detonate at 7-card hand (12 potential dmg) into an UNSEEN armed Absorb. Fully prevented + opponent healed 6 (10hp -> 16hp). Searing Riposte was NOT funded/armed that turn, so no punishment triggered. This single event cost ~18 effective HP of tempo and is the biggest strategic error of the match — should have probed with a cheap spell first that turn, or kept Searing Riposte armed at all times as insurance per doctrine.
- R10-R14: Reckoning fired 4 times (6+6+4+4=20 damage to me) while I was simultaneously chipping through ward after ward that fed the SAME lifetime-prevented bank Reckoning reads. The ledger-spender family (Warding Tithe, Restoring Rune) DECREMENTED that bank when they spent it (visibly reduced the 3rd/4th Reckoning to 4 instead of 6+), but Abjuration cast Reckoning EVERY round it could regardless, out-racing my burst.
- R13 T97: Correctly raced with Meteor (7 dmg, my only funded spell) while at 6 HP rather than passing — got opponent to 5 HP, survived one more turn by luck (they chose to heal with Restoring Rune instead of Reckoning that cycle).
- Final: opponent chose Reckoning at 7 HP into my 2 HP — any nonzero damage was lethal; I had no counter available.

HYPOTHESIS VERDICT: Bank-starving got LESS effective, not more, once the ledger family showed up — but not for the reason expected. The spenders (Tithe/Rune) DO decrement the bank as documented (Reckoning damage visibly shrank from 6 to 4 as they spent on Tithe/Rune between casts), so starving the bank IS real and IS working exactly as designed. The problem is asymmetric: Abjuration can choose EVERY round whether to spend the bank on defense/healing (shrinking future Reckoning) or nuke with Reckoning now, while Evocation has no lever to force that choice — my chip damage feeds the bank regardless of whether I want it to (every ward soak counts), and I can't meaningfully stop them casting Reckoning when they're ready. Effectively: my own sustained pressure was directly fueling the wall that killed me. Kill-before-R9 remains correct in THEORY but this game shows it's fragile against Restoring Rune's repeated resets (opponent bounced off single digits 3 separate times) — Evocation needs either (a) a way to close out a round where the opponent is <10 HP with an UNCONDITIONAL alpha that isn't reaction-able, or (b) to stop feeding the bank entirely once Reckoning is online (level 10+), favoring Dispelling-Powder-style ward removal (no bank credit) and pierce/Doom effects over face-routing damage that ALWAYS goes through wards first.

LEDGER-FAMILY TELEMETRY:
- Warding Tithe (ABJ-046) cast at least 5 times, consistently converting 4 HP wards from the bank.
- Restoring Rune (ABJ-048) cast 3 times, healing 3, 3, and 3 HP each (spending 6 from bank each time — bank clearly had room).
- Sealed Verdict (ABJ-047) was prepped repeatedly but never actually cast/observed firing in this game — opponent never needed it since Absorb and Phase Shift handled my big spells.
- Reckoning (ABJ-032) fired 4 times total: 6, 6, 4, 4 (final, lethal). The decreasing values across the match confirm the ledger-decrement mechanic is real and visible — it just wasn't enough to save me since Abjuration simply cast it every round it had SS available regardless of size.
- Absorb (ABJ-011, not technically "ledger family" but same wall) ate one big Detonate (12 dmg fully denied + 6 heal) and one small Crackle+Battle-Trance combo (4 dmg denied + 2 heal) — it fires on ANY targeted spell the bot deems worth reacting to, not just big ones, meaning baiting with cheap spells does NOT reliably exhaust it before a real alpha strike (it ate the small one instead of the big one on separate occasions, unpredictably).
- Phase Shift (ABJ-014) fully cancelled one Fireball outright (no damage, no bank feed) — a clean, repeatable answer to single spells that bypasses the bank-starving strategy entirely since it isn't a prevention/absorb effect (Searing Riposte, which triggers on damage prevention/reduction, would NOT have punished a cancel).

BOT BLIND SPOTS / OBSERVATIONS:
- Greedy bot correctly identified Detonate as absorb-worthy but ALSO absorbed a trivial 4-damage Crackle+Battle-Trance later — its Absorb-timing heuristic seems to react to any targeted spell rather than reserving for genuinely large threats, which is somewhat exploitable (you cannot safely predict when it'll pop) but also means baiting doesn't reliably work either.
- Bot correctly alternated between Reckoning (nuke) and Restoring Rune (sustain) based on its own HP, choosing to heal at 5 HP instead of nuking — reasonable defensive AI.
- No rule bugs observed. Burn markers, Reflective Ward's prevention-triggered counter-damage, and Crackle's split ward/face targeting all behaved consistently with their card text once matched against ward-routing precedent.
