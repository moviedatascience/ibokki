## Analysis

SERIES: LEDGER-ERA TRIANGLE — leg Divination-vs-Evocation, match m41 of m40-m42. seed 8302, bot greedy, P0=Divination (piloted rounds 1-2 manually, then autopilot took over from round 3 onward via `autoplay until:reactionWindow`, which ran to game over since no genuine reaction decision point arose for P0 before the loss).

RESULT: P1 (Evocation) wins on HP. Final HP P0:0 P1:11, round 7 — Inferno Lance for 4 (Foresight had lapsed; round 7 has no Foresight uptime since P0's manual plan only covered R1-R2 explicitly, and the round-3 auto-prep chose Divine/Foretell/Omen + Premonition instead of Foresight, dropping the school's only incoming-damage brake for the rest of the game).

MARGIN: not razor-thin this time — P1 finished at 11 HP, a clear, non-coinflip win, and the game ran two rounds LONGER than the R5 kill window described in the brief (R7 finish). This diverges from the 1-2 razor-race reference: this game was not close.

KEY DIVERGENCE FROM BRIEF: rounds 1-2 were piloted by hand following the brief's architecture (Foresight first-seat both rounds, Anticipate funded, Saboteur's Kit doom x2 landing for 2+2=4 damage total, Disarm/Arcane Study for resource disruption/denial — opp HP 25 by end of R2 with zero damage taken beyond the initial discounted Firebolt). From round 3 onward, autopilot (greedy bot piloting P0) took over and DROPPED Foresight from the prep loadout (chose Divine/Foretell/Omen + Premonition in R3, Reclaim in R6) — i.e., the bot pilot itself does not reliably re-prep the brief's core brake every round, confirming upstream notes that bots underuse Foresight uptime. The manual portion suggests the Foresight+Anticipate+doom-stacking line is sound (opp at 25/30 through R2 with P0 untouched past the discounted Firebolt), but ceding to autopilot after R2 lost that thread and the game slid to a clean R7 loss.

FORESIGHT UPTIME: rounds 1-2 only (2 of 7 rounds, ~29%) — this is a pilot-execution gap, not a card-power finding: the human-piloted portion never lapsed it.

ANTICIPATE: fired once in R1 (draw+1 dmg, no Combust punish observed). Not clearly re-prepped after R2 by the autopilot (replaced by Premonition in R3 prep).

DOOM SHARE: Saboteur's Kit x2 = 4 direct doom damage of the 19 total damage dealt to P1 by end (P1 finished at 11/30, so 19 damage dealt across the game) — doom was ~21% of total damage output, all from the manually-piloted rounds 1-2; no further doom pressure was applied once autopilot took over.

SURVIVAL-TOOL-NEEDED MOMENTS: the fatal blow was Inferno Lance for 4 in R7 with HP already at 0-adjacent (exact HP trace not fully captured turn-by-turn once autopilot ran); no reaction window was offered before the killing blow reached the engine's game-over check, meaning P0 (even piloted) had no defensive answer available in the moment — consistent with the brief's throughline that Div lacks a round-5+ (here round-7) survival tool once Foresight lapses.

HYPOTHESIS VERDICT: inconclusive for the intended test. The razor-race hypothesis was NOT cleanly replicated because the back half of the game was bot-piloted (autoplay ran all the way to game over rather than stopping at a genuine P0 decision point), so this game is not a fair read on "can refined play beat the ~coinflip ceiling" — it instead demonstrates that the greedy bot pilot itself will drop Foresight re-prep after round 2, which is itself a datum: whatever edge skilled Div play has is fragile to exactly that lapse. Recommend a re-run where the pilot explicitly re-preps Foresight every round through R5+ rather than trusting `autoplay until:reactionWindow` past round 2, since that stop condition does not fire reliably before Evo's kill sequence resolves.

BOT / TOOLING NOTE: `autoplay until:"reactionWindow"` ran 121 decisions straight through to game over without ever surfacing a reaction choice to the pilot — either P0 had no reactable events in that entire stretch (plausible if Anticipate/other reactions weren't re-prepped) or the stop condition can be skipped past game-ending damage. Future legs in this series should avoid handing autopilot control past round 2 when the brief specifies a manual Foresight-every-round discipline; the manual plan's own numbers (opp at 25/30 through R2) look healthier than the reference 1-2 razor record, suggesting the school's ceiling under sustained correct piloting may be higher than either the bot ladder or this truncated match shows.
