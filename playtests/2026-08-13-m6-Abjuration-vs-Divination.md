## Analysis

## Result: ABJURATION WINS, round 13. Final HP: Abj 19, Div -28 (Ward Collapse for 42, overkill 28). Seed 2102, greedy Div bot at full strength.

Context: bot-level this matchup is 30-0 Divination (avg 10.2 rounds). Human-grade piloting won game 1 of 1, and never dropped below 19 HP. The matchup is not just winnable — with the right plan it felt LOPSIDED in Abjuration's favor by midgame.

### What actually won it (in order of importance)
1. **Counterbind deterrence, not Counterbind cancels.** One cancel was actually spent all game (Reclaim, R7). The armed [SM] on ABJ-015 froze the greedy bot almost completely from R5 onward — it would fuel its whole board and then pass every turn rather than cast into an open cancel. Entire rounds passed where Div did literally nothing. This is a bot blindspot worth logging: greedy values "spell cancelled" as bad enough to prefer zero action forever, letting me bank cards, chip, and level for free. Its hoarded fuel then SWEPT at round end (I controlled round end by exhausting my own cast slots) — 4-6 component cards of theirs vaporized per round several times.
2. **Stonewarden hard-blanks the Div L1 kit.** "Cannot be targeted by Level 1 spells" turned off Foretell, Cut the Thread AND Omen — verified live when Cut the Thread FIZZLED (engine message: untargetable). From R6 the only damage Div ever did to me was Saboteur's Kit gambit dooms (2 every ~2 rounds) and my own reshuffle exhaustion. One 2HP ward = permanent immunity to ~80% of their book. Likely a balance problem: L1-lockout on an SS spell with a persistent ward, against a school whose damage base is all L1.
3. **Ward Collapse + 12 rounds of Fortify/Ward Pulse/Bulwark = a 42-damage one-shot.** Since Div could never touch my wards, every +2 Fortify was permanent stored damage. The "ward HP is wasted vs their tiny chip" intuition inverted at lv10: the big ward became a bomb. Detonated turn 104 for 42 vs their 14 HP.
4. **Ward Pulse chip + exhaustion damage** did the other half: 10 Pulse casts (1/round) + Shieldbreaker Pulse 4 + their reshuffle penalty (2) brought them 30→14 before the bomb.

### Tool scorecard
- Runic Seal: 3 casts, all on Omen (R3, R8, R9 — the R9 one stranded their attached MM fuel). Denied ~6 pierce. Fine early, redundant after Stonewarden; swapped out R13.
- Counterbind: armed ~6 rounds, spent once (Reclaim). Deterrence value >> cancel value.
- Dooms eaten: Omen x3 (6), Saboteur's Kit x4 (8) = 14 total pierce taken; Second Wind refunded 5. Entropy was prepped by them at R12 but NEVER cast — CB deterrence again.
- Stonewarden: 2 casts; blanked Foretell/Cut the Thread/Omen from R6 onward.
- Ward Collapse: 1 cast, 42 damage, game.
- Shieldbreaker Pulse (late swap-in): 1 cast, 4 damage. Would have been the backup wincon (5+/round with Pulse).
- Reckoning/Final Reckoning: NEVER USED. Bank was ~4 prevented all game (they couldn't hit my wards, so nothing charged it) and L4 never unlocked by round 13 (tier gates: L2@lv5, L3@lv10 — the briefing's "L3@7 / FR@9-10" is wrong for this build; L4 is lv14+).
- Sentinel Rune: good VS-sink, ~2 bonus draws + Pulse bodies. Grounding: 1 prevent+draw. Both fine, not load-bearing.

### Their exhaustion: 1 reshuffle (2 dmg) at R8; second never arrived because freezing also stopped their draw engine spells. I reshuffled once (2 dmg).

### Rules learned the hard way (log for future pilots)
- **2-component-card cap per spell**: SSS is only fundable as SS+S — three bare S cards can NEVER cast it. Cost shapes matter more than pip totals; I sat on a lethal Collapse for 3 rounds waiting for the SS-card draw.
- Round ends only when a side EXHAUSTS its cast slots (other side gets one final turn) — a player who refuses to cast can stall a round indefinitely; I used my own slot-exhaustion as a round-end lever to sweep their hoarded fuel and control level-ups.
- Detach returns the component to hand (free), so reaction fuel can be re-banked... only before round end; attached fuel sweeps.
- Gambit-sourced dooms (Saboteur's Kit) bypass both counterspells and Stonewarden — the only unanswerable damage in the matchup.

### Verdict
Abj vs Div at human level: WINNABLE and arguably favored. The load-bearing tools, in order: **Counterbind (as a standing threat), Stonewarden (L1 lockout), Ward Collapse (wards-as-battery wincon)**. The briefed wincons (Reckoning/Final Reckoning) were irrelevant: Reckoning can't charge against an opponent who can't hit you, and FR's tier never unlocks on this clock — Ward Collapse at L3 is THE Abjuration closer.

### Balance flags
- Greedy bot freeze vs armed cancels: pathological passivity; it should discount deterrence when its own clock (exhaustion) is losing, or bait/overload the single reaction. This, more than card power, is why bot-Abj goes 0-30: bot Abj never keeps a cancel armed, and bot Div exploits nothing.
- Stonewarden vs Div: one SS spell permanently blanks Foretell/Omen/Cut the Thread (their L1 damage core). Feels mispriced/miswired — consider making dooms resolve as non-targeted, or the immunity only while the specific 2HP ward survives AND making Div chip able to hit wards past it.
- Ward Collapse + untouchable wards: unbounded stored damage. Div has zero ward interaction in the pool seen; any school without ward removal auto-loses the long game to this line.
- Quenching Salts is a dead card vs burn-less schools (drew 3, all dead).
- Omen MM for 2 pierce is very weak vs any seal/cancel economy; it was the whole early Div plan and cost them 2 cards/round of fuel.
