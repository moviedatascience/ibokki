BOT BLIND SPOT / BUG FOUND: Fortress [ABJ-029] card text reads "Wards you control cannot be targeted or destroyed by your opponent this round" (blanket, all wards) but the implementation (`createWardForSelfWith(4,{protected:true})`, packages/engine/src/effects/abjuration.ts:55) only flags the NEWLY CREATED ward as protected — it does not retroactively protect existing wards. Concretely: I cast Fortress with an 8 HP ward already up; Prophecy of Collapse still destroyed the 8 HP ward (unprotected) that same round while sparing only the new 4 HP Fortress ward. This is a genuine text/implementation mismatch worth fixing (either the text should say "the Ward you create" or the effect should retroactively flag all current wards).

Second, milder observation: Fortify's implicit ward target is the OLDEST ward (array index 0), not the largest — this occasionally inflates an already-large ward into an even juicier Prophecy of Collapse target. Not a bug, but a trap for careless piloting (I walked into it once with an 8 HP ward baited and destroyed for nothing, which is fine since it was free-earned HP, but worth knowing).

Third: multi-symbol component math matters a lot for high-cost late spells. Every prepared spell has a hard 2-card attachment cap (packages/engine/src/legal.ts: `prep.attached.length >= 2`), so a cost like SSSM (Final Reckoning) can ONLY be paid by exactly two component cards whose symbols sum to that multiset (e.g. CMP-SS + CMP-SM). I wasted two turns attaching lone CMP-S cards to it before recognizing this — a real skill/knowledge gate for pilots, not a bug, but worth flagging as a UX trap (the attach list doesn't warn that adding a 3rd card is capped, it simply omits the option silently).
