## Analysis

**RESULT**: P0 (Divination) wins by HP at Round 6, Turn 45. Final HP: P0 21, P1 -1 (killed by an Omen doom tick). Margin: 21 HP — far more comfortable than either m1-m4 reference win (2 HP, 6 HP).

**PER-CARD CONTRIBUTION**
- Omen (DIV-012): recast every round R1-R6 without fail, the backbone of the clock — landed 2 dmg × 6 = 12 damage total, including the final killing blow.
- Foreclosure (DIV-020): swapped in for Insight at R5 once L2 unlocked. One cast, landed for 4 (12→8hp swing). Confirmed as advertised — a big, reliable delayed chunk once available.
- Foretell (DIV-011): cast every round it got budget (R2-R4, R6) for 2 immediate dmg each + hand intel. The intel was the standout value this game — revealed the opponent's V-heavy hand every time, explaining the brick in real time rather than leaving it a mystery.
- Anticipate (DIV-014): funded most rounds but only actually fired ONCE all game (R5 T40, reacting to Searing Word, 1 dmg + draw). Confirmed the documented "no arm window on opponent's first cast when they have first seat" gap cost at least one fire (R5's Inferno Lance opener). Overall a much weaker contributor than the doom spells this game specifically because the opponent so rarely cast anything to react to.
- Premonition (DIV-005): cast R3 and R4, found a 2nd Saboteur's Kit copy plus assorted M/SM components — matches playbook's "found free Saboteur's Kits" pattern exactly.
- Saboteur's Kit (GAM-019, gambit): played twice (R2, R3) for 2 free doom-clock damage each, completely outside the cast/turn budget. Highest damage-per-action-spent card in the deck — bypasses both the stack and the 1-cast/turn and 3-cast/round caps.
- Disarm (GAM-020, gambit) / Scrying Lens (ITM-001, item): minor value, mostly informational/tempo, not decisive.
- Sealed Vault / Mana Sickness: held the whole game, never played — no round in this match ever needed them; deck never got thin enough (ended at 7 cards remaining) and the opponent was already so bricked that denying draws seemed low-value.

**EVO PRESSURE ASSESSMENT**: The improved bot's clock was dramatically WEAKER than the m1-m4 reference here, not stronger. The opponent cast literally zero spells across Rounds 1-4 (four full rounds), first casting anything at R5 (Inferno Lance, 4 dmg) after already being at 12 HP. `Foretell`'s hand-reveals showed why every time: an extremely Verbal-heavy hand (7-8 of 10 cards V-only or V-heavy: CMP-V/VV/VSM) with no Material/Somatic to complete costs. This does not read as the "faster, less passive Evo" the brief predicted from the tier-1/2 slot-waste and sweep-fuel changes — if anything this looks like a severe draw-luck outlier for this specific seed (5103) rather than a representative sample of the improved instrument. By the time Evo started casting (R5-R6: Inferno Lance 4, Hex Bolt 4(!), Searing Word 1), Divination's doom-clock lead was already insurmountable (opponent at 12 before their first cast landed).

**VERDICT ON THE EDGE**: The doom-clock line still wins comfortably against the current, stronger greedy instrument — but this particular game is NOT a clean re-validation of the racing matchup because the opponent's near-total brick through R1-R4 makes it closer to a bye than a race. The 21 HP margin should be read with real caution: it is inflated by an apparent seed-specific component-drought, not purely by the improved doom-clock execution outpacing a functioning Evo curve. Recommend treating this game as inconclusive on "does the line still beat a genuinely curving Evo" and re-running with a different seed (or checking whether seed 5103 produces this same opening hand skew for Evo across bot levels) before updating the edge verdict.

**PRICING NOTES**: Hex Bolt (EVO-012, VV cost, "Deal 3 damage, cannot be the target of Reactions") actually dealt 4 in this game (Empowered Chalk item boost, presumably) — worth a sanity check that Reaction-immunity text combined with item boosts isn't creating a card that's simultaneously undodgeable and bigger than its base text implies. Saboteur's Kit remains extremely efficient (2 free uncancellable pierce dmg, no stack exposure, doesn't touch either budget) — if it isn't already flagged as a high-priority always-play in bot heuristic valuation, it should be.

**BOT BLINDSPOTS**: The standout finding is the opponent's 4-round dead brick (T2 through T41, zero spells cast) purely from what Foretell's reveals showed was a V-symbol flood with no M/S. Whether this is (a) bad luck intrinsic to seed 5103's shuffle, (b) a deck-construction issue in the default Evocation preset skewing too many pure-V cards into the curve, or (c) a bot mulligan-policy gap (bot mulliganed down to 3 cards in R1 and STILL ended up V-flooded — the mulligan didn't fix the problem) is worth a follow-up look. Given CLAUDE.md's own pilot-gap doctrine, this game's 100%-esque blowout is a signal to check the OTHER direction too, not just declare Div stronger — a seed sweep or a check of the Evocation preset's V/M/S component ratio would clarify whether this is representative.
