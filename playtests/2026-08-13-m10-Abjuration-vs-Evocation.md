## Analysis

## Piloted calibration: Abjuration (Claude) vs Evocation (greedy bot), seed 3103

**RESULT: WIN, round 10. Final HP 29–0.** Reckoning #1 hit for 22 on the first turn it was legal (level 10); Ward Pulse ping closed the last point two turns later. I took 4 face damage all game (rounds 1–2, before the engine was assembled) and healed 3 back.

### Stone Stance exchange rate
9 casts (every round 1–9; R10's SS fuel went to Reckoning instead). Estimated damage prevented by stance alone: ~12–14. Honest reading: stance's per-cast rate was mediocre THIS game (~1.5/cast) because the greedy bot front-loads its casts first-seat before stance is up, and spent two whole rounds (8–9) in V-drought casting nothing. Stance still mattered: it zeroed every Spark/Burning Hands and blunted Firebolt/Hex Bolt/Lance mid-round, and it double-dips by feeding the Reckoning bank. But the fat ward, not stance, was the main banker here.

### Reckoning bank progression
~4 by R2, ~10 by R3, ~14 by R4, ~23 by R5, ~28 by R6, ~33 by R7, ~37 by R8 (unchanged R9), ~44 after Inferno+burn soaks in R10 → cast dealt 22 (engine confirms soaks count: card text "Damage your Wards absorb counts as prevented"). One cast, first legal round, 96% of their HP bar. The historical 92% WR-used number is not a fluke; on this edge Reckoning is a metronome: survive to R10 with wards up and it one-shots.

### Ward economy vs chip
Ward soaks were the engine: ~28 HP absorbed across the match (Firebolt 1+3, burn ticks, Hex Bolt 1, Fireball 5+5, Lance 3, Combust 4, Inferno 5). Sources: Fortify x5 (+10), Ward Pulse x5 (+6 across two wards), Arcane Shell (+1), Bulwark Shard x2 (+4), Aegis Charm (+3). Evo chip never came close to breaking through — wards entered every round ≥3HP and I ended at 29/30. Key insight: **every point Evo deals into an Abj ward is half a point of Reckoning damage later — the aggro plan literally charges the defender's wincon.** Ward Pulse also supplied 6 kill-relevant chip (5 pings + the finisher).

### Reactions — mine and theirs
Mine: Grounding x2 (2 prevented, 2 draws — fine), Absorb x1 (ate Inferno Lance whole, +4 bank +2 heal — excellent), Echo Shield 0 uses (prepped R4–R10, never needed; stance+ward already zeroed the windows it would cover — candidate for "prep something else"). Theirs: **Combust fired in response to my Stone Stance SPELL cast (R8) — its text reads "When your opponent plays a Reaction". Likely engine bug (proxy/SIMPLIFIED trigger?); grep the Combust implementation.** It dealt 4 into my ward pre-stance-resolution, i.e. it fed my bank. Searing Riposte/Backdraft/Volatile Bolt never appeared.

### Where the greedy bot punished or surprised
- R1: Dispelling Powder destroyed Stone Stance before Firebolt — genuinely sharp counterplay, the only time all game the bot answered my engine rather than racing it. It never drew/used a second one.
- Searing Word cast twice into a hand I'd deliberately emptied of components (attach-first discipline) — whiffed both times.
- R9 blindspot: bot fully fueled Inferno Lance [VV] AND Combust [VV], then passed its final turn, let all four V's sweep, and swapped Lance out of prep next round. An entire loaded round thrown away. Combined with R8 (zero spell casts), the bot went two consecutive rounds without damage during my banking phase.

### Verdict on the edge
At skilled play this matchup is decisively Abjuration-favored — the 53% bot-vs-bot number badly understates it. The Evo bot's whole-game raw output (~35) was almost fully metabolized by stance/wards/Absorb into a 44-point prevention bank, and Reckoning converts that bank into a one-turn kill at R10 with zero setup beyond "keep doing your defensive gameplan". Evo's only real levers were Dispelling Powder (hits stance, not wards) and hand-attack (dodged by attaching). To make this competitive Evo needs either ward-piercing reach (Lightning Bolt's floor-1 was the right idea; it never cast it) or pressure that doesn't feed the bank.

### Mispricing flags
- **Reckoning (SS, L3)** feels overtuned into aggro: ceil(prevented/2) with ward soaks counting means any deck that attacks a warding Abj guarantees a ~20+ nuke on R10 for two Somatics. Consider: exclude ward soaks, halve-round-down with a cap, or make it VSM/SSS.
- **Ward Pulse (S, L1)** is quietly excellent (+1/ward multi-ward scaling + repeatable ping); fine at one ward, strong at two.
- **Combust** — verify trigger condition in engine (fired on a Spell cast).
- Evo bot valuation: it hoarded V-fuel to sweep twice and led spike spells first-seat into an 8HP ward; its sequencing, not its cards, lost the race here.
