## Analysis

RESULT: P0 Divination (piloted) WINS in round 5. Final HP: Div 2, Evo -1. Seed 907 vs greedy bot. Mirrors the seed-812 game almost exactly (Div won R5 at 2 HP there too) — the playbook reproduces.

PER-CARD CONTRIBUTION (31 total dmg dealt; 29 by me + 2 Battle Trance self-damage):
- Dooms = 14 dmg (45% of the kill): Saboteur's Kit x2 (2+2), Omen x3 casts (2+2+2), Foreclosure (4, the killshot). A doom was ticking in every round except a 1-turn gap in R3/R4.
- Foretell: cast all 5 rounds, 10 dmg + hand intel (caught their R2 hand = GAM-010 only → pressed the drought).
- Anticipate: fired all 5 rounds without fail = 5 dmg + 5 draws; the draws delivered fuel twice at exactly the right time (R3 CMP-M, R5 CMP-M).
- Foresight: 1 cast (R4). Saved ~2 HP (Spark 2→1, Burning Hands 1→0) + scry-3-take-1 fetched fuel. Skipped it in R5 — correct: Foreclosure had to go down on my first R5 turn for the checkmate timing.
- Premonition: 2 casts, 5 cards, including the 2nd Saboteur's Kit (R3) — draw engine earned its slot before the L5 Foreclosure swap.
- Scrying Lens: dug CMP-MM during the R3 fuel drought — quietly pivotal.
- Disarm (GAM-020), Mana Sickness (GAM-017), Sealed Vault (GAM-016): never used. Bot's hand was almost always 0, so Disarm was dead; Mana Sickness is a no-op vs this bot (it only draws its 1 normal draw/turn).

EVO BRICK ASSESSMENT: Real and material. R2 the bot passed an entire turn with hand 0 and managed only 1 cast that round while both my dooms fired into it (took 4+2 doom over R2). That round bought me the race margin. R3-R5 it never bricked again (3 casts/round) and its L5 spike (Inferno Lance 4 + burn) nearly closed me out — I was at 4 entering R5 and won at 2.

NEW MECHANICAL LEARNINGS (not in the brief):
1. Burn is a ONE-SHOT delayed tick (fires once at end of an opponent turn, then expires — status flag clears), not a persistent per-round DoT. It also applies even when Foresight reduces Burning Hands' damage to 0.
2. Two single CMP-M cards stack on one spell to pay an MM cost ([M,M] on Foreclosure) — you don't need a CMP-MM card.
3. One-spell-per-TURN is enforced strictly: after casting spell A, a second cast the same turn is rejected even with round cast-slots remaining (learned by rejected slug, no damage done).
4. Searing Word is base 1 dmg + discard; its scary 4-dmg R2 hit was Battle Trance (+2, 2 self-dmg) + Empowered Chalk stacking. Greedy happily buff-stacks its weakest spell.
5. The greedy Evo bot prepped zero reactions all game and never held components across turns (draws 1/turn, attaches, casts immediately) — reaction risk before casting was nil, and hand-denial cards are dead vs it.
6. Doom checkmate geometry confirmed end-to-end: Foreclosure cast on my first R5 turn ticked their T2, fired at their T3 START — they died holding a legal 3rd cast they never got to make.

SKILL / LUCK / CARD-POWER READ: Mostly playbook skill — doom-always-ticking, Anticipate every round, banking hand fuel across rounds (attachments sweep, hand doesn't), and the R4 decision to take Foresight+bank-fuel over a faster Omen line (which would have left me at ~5 HP into the L5 spike; I hit R5 at 9 and still only survived at 2). Luck contributed: the R2 bot brick, Premonition finding Saboteur's Kit #2, and their R5 second cast being Burning Hands (1) instead of Hex Bolt (3) — though the line survived even the Hex Bolt branch (would have ended at 1 HP). Card power: Foreclosure is the win condition, full stop; without the L5 swap the race doesn't close. Two piloted wins now, both ending at exactly 2 HP in R5 — the matchup is winnable with discipline but has zero slack; the sim bots' 0-240 is a valuation gap (they can't see doom checkmate timing), not a hopeless matchup.
