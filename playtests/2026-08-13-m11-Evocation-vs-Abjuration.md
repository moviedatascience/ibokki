## Analysis

PILOTED CALIBRATION: Evocation (me) vs greedy Abjuration bot, seed 3201. RESULT: WIN, round 8, HP 19 vs -4. The Reckoning window (level 10 = R10) was never reached — kill landed two rounds early.

== BRIEFING CORRECTIONS (engine facts learned in-game, all verified in the log) ==
1. Burn ticks do NOT bypass Stone Stance in this build. R3: "P1 burns for 1" with stance up → 0 damage, marker consumed. Stance's "spell damage -2" covers burn ticks. Wards also absorb burn ticks (R2: tick destroyed their ward instead of hitting face). The burn playbook vs a stancing Abj is dead tech.
2. Reaction damage IS spell damage: Stone Stance reduced Searing Riposte's 2 to 0 (R3) — so Backdraft/Riposte are blanked in stance rounds too.
3. Searing Riposte does NOT trigger on ward absorption — only on explicit prevent/reduce effects. Vs Abj it's a net bank-feeder (its trigger charges their bank via stance, its output dies to the same stance). Cut it R4.
4. Crackle's ward-targeted 2 pierces Stone Stance (wall 7→5 during a stance round). Best repeatable wall tech.
5. Attachment sweep is symmetric and is the tactical heart of this matchup: neither side can hold armed reactions across rounds.

== HOW THE WIN ACTUALLY HAPPENED ==
Strategic frame: REFUSE TO FEED THE BANK. Every point of mine that died on stance/wards = 0.5 Reckoning damage later, so damage into mitigation is worse than no damage. Rounds 3-5 I cast almost nothing (one Crackle per round at most), hoarded to hand cap, and let the bot burn 3 casts + items per round building walls against zero pressure. Then two alpha strikes in windows where (a) I moved first and (b) their reaction fuel had just swept:

== VERDICT ON THE EDGE ==
At skilled play this edge flips hard: EVO favored, and not close (19-HP margin, kill at R8 vs a wincon that starts at R10). The bot-vs-bot 53/47 Abj edge exists because Evo bots cast damage every round into stance+wards, continuously charging Reckoning and paying the stance tax — they play the matchup Abj wants. A pilot plays a different game entirely: silence during stance rounds, fuel hoarding (hand persistence + 10 cap), free-spell wall removal, and sweep-window alphas. The greedy bot cannot punish refusal: its stance is wasted S vs a non-caster, its armed cancels evaporate every round end, and its ward EHP only becomes Reckoning fuel if the opponent cooperates.
Bot blindspots exposed: (1) casts Stone Stance vs zero incoming pressure; (2) over-arms reactions that predictably sweep; (3) spends SS cancels on 1-damage utility; (4) no plan vs a hoard-then-Detonate line; (5) never pressured my life total (no clock = denial is free).

== PRICING NOTES ==
- Detonate (VV, L2): with hand cap 10 and hand persistence, it's a 14-20 damage one-card wincon vs turtles. Feels mispriced/miscapped — consider "discard up to X" or per-component scaling.
- Dispelling Powder (free Gambit): deletes an entire stacked ward (6+ EHP AND its Reckoning conversion) for zero cost/zero cast slot. Disproportionate vs Abj's whole plan.
- Stone Stance (S, L1): -2 to ALL opposing damage including burn ticks and reactions, round-long, while also charging prevention... as written in this engine it blanks Evo's whole L1 kit. Only its S-cost-per-round and refusal-play keep it fair.
- Searing Riposte: near-dead vs the school it seems designed against (stance eats its own trigger output). 
- Battle Trance/Overclock/Empowered Chalk: excellent, fairly costed burst enablers — they made the alpha windows lethal.
- Burn package (Kindle/Burning Hands/Inferno/Wildfire): if design intent is "burn bypasses mitigation" (per prior playtest doctrine), the engine disagrees — stance reduces ticks and wards absorb them. Either the engine or the doctrine needs a decision here; as-is burn is unplayable vs Abjuration.
