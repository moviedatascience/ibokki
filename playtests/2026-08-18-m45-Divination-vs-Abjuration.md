## Analysis

SERIES: EXP-8 SHATTER CALIBRATION (2026-08-18), m45 of m45-m47, Div(P0) vs Abj(P1), seed 8401, bot greedy, controls=0 (fully manual/piloted throughout).

RESULT: P0 (Divination) wins, round 10, HP 27 vs -1. Total dominance — Abjuration's bank/wall engine never got off the ground.

HYPOTHESIS VERDICTS:
1. BANK STARVING — STRONGLY CONFIRMED. By refusing to cast direct-damage spells (Foretell/Omen) into existing wards and instead holding them for windows when P1 had zero wards, P1's total prevented-damage bank stayed under ~6 for the entire 10-round game. Warding Tithe (min(4,bank)) never produced more than a 3 HP ward. Reckoning was prepped once (R10) but never cast — bank was too thin to matter. Bot-Abj's whole wall economy is entirely dependent on the attacker feeding it; a disciplined attacker just... doesn't, and the engine starves to death.

2. SHATTER ECONOMICS — CONFIRMED, but the even bigger discovery was DIV-004 Prophecy of Collapse and DIV-019 Unbind are FREE (zero damage, pure "destroy") ward removal — no bank credit at all, since no damage is dealt. These two cards trivialized every ward P1 built: 2HP, 3HP, 5HP wards were all deleted at zero cost across the game (confirmed via card text: Prophecy of Collapse's inscribed doom explicitly showed "0 damage in 2 turns" in the log). This is arguably a bigger lever than the shatter-on-touch rule for doom damage — the shatter rule matters when you WANT to also deal face damage; the pure-destroy tools matter when you just want the wall gone with total bank denial.

3. Prophecy of Collapse was scheduled repeatedly and never whiffed once P1 had any ward — free removal every single time, and dynamically retargeted "largest ward at fire time" so pre-casting it early was safe.

4. Absorb showed up once (reacting to my Anticipate reaction's 1 damage) — confirms the brief's claim that Absorb only answers CAST spells, never firing dooms (Omen/Saboteur's Kit/Prophecy of Collapse fired repeatedly with zero Absorb interaction). Phase Shift and Interrupt (spell-cancel reactions) were prepped by P1 but never actually used against my casts — bot-Abj held them defensively and never found a good window, which combined with my doom-heavy plan (dooms can't be cancelled once they've already resolved as a cast+inscribe, only the initial cast could theoretically be Phase-Shifted, and the bot never tried).

5. Reckoning/Sealed Verdict/Final Reckoning never got cast — the bank never reached their thresholds (Sealed Verdict needs 6, Reckoning likely scales off bank too). The L4 "long game" clock never mattered because the game ended R10.

DOOM CLOCK VS WALL: doom clock won decisively and early. Direct damage (Foretell 2, Omen 2 doom, Saboteur's Kit 2 doom — the latter is a FREE gambit that bypasses the one-spell-per-turn limit entirely) plus free ward removal (Prophecy of Collapse, Unbind) meant P1 took chip damage almost every single turn from round 2 onward with almost no wards ever surviving to absorb it. P1 dropped 30->28->26->24->22->20->18->16->14->12->10->8->6->4(ish)->1->dead across rounds, a remarkably steady ~2-3 HP/turn bleed that a wall-based deck simply cannot out-race when the wall keeps evaporating for free.

BOT BLIND SPOTS / SUSPECTED ISSUES:
- Bot-Abj built wards it knew (or should have known) it couldn't afford (Warding Tithe when bank≈0 → 0-2 HP wards) and kept re-investing gambits/items (Aegis Charm, Bulwark Shard, Reflective Ward) into single wards that got deleted next turn by my already-scheduled Prophecy of Collapse/Unbind. It never seemed to value-adjust against a visible removal threat.
- Bot-Abj held Interrupt/Phase Shift the entire game without ever using them, despite me casting spells virtually every turn it could have targeted. Possible bot passivity/blind-spot on proactive reaction usage for non-lethal-looking casts.
- Deck exhaustion mechanic: both sides took automatic damage (2 dmg for me at R6, 2 dmg for P1 at R7) when their 40-card resource deck ran out and reshuffled discard back in. This is a real, non-trivial clock nobody discusses in the design brief — worth flagging as a secondary pressure valve independent of doom/wards.
- UI/tooling note (not a game bug): attach-target slugs for identical component types are NOT stably indexed to prep slot position — e.g. "att-cmp-mm-2" sometimes meant "3rd distinct mm-card instance, slot index 2" rather than "slot 2." I mis-attached to the wrong prepared slot roughly 6 times this game and had to detach/retry. This cost tool calls but no strategic damage since I always caught it via the resulting board state before passing.
