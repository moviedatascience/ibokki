RESULT: P0 (Abjuration) wins at Round 14, HP 25 vs -3 (Reckoning for 12 finished it). Comfortable win with zero close calls — HP never dropped below 25 after round 9.

### Bank trajectory
Fed by: ward absorption on shatter (2, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 HP wards destroyed over the game), Stone Stance's flat -2 reduction on chip dooms (which — key finding below — often fires BEFORE damage ever reaches a ward), and full blocks of Saboteur's Kit/Far Sight via Stone Stance alone.
Spent on: one early Warding Tithe (spent 4 for a 4HP bumper, R3). Two Reckoning casts: R10 for 7 dmg (bank ~14), R13 for 12 dmg (bank ~24), R14 for 12 dmg (lethal). Reckoning (non-consuming, per the brief) was cast three times total and was the actual win condition — the wall never needed to attack, it just cashed the ledger.

### Shatter events (every ward destruction)
1. R1: 2HP ward vs Saboteur's Kit(2) — exact match, banked 2, zero waste.
2. R2: 2HP ward vs Prophecy of Collapse (direct destroy, no damage) — 0 banked, ward wasted (Collapse ignores sizing entirely, it's a hard destroy not damage-routed).
3. R3-R8: repeated pattern — Collapse kept sniping my "largest ward" the instant I rebuilt it to 4HP (the Foreclosure-sized bumper), banking 0, before Foreclosure itself could ever profit from a full-size ward. This is the central tension: Collapse and Foreclosure share a caster and both threaten the ward layer, and Collapse always got there first because it has a 2-turn fuse vs my 1-turn Fortify rebuild cadence.
4. R7 (T49): 6HP ward vs Far Sight(2) — **no shatter happened at all**. HP, wards, and log all showed zero change. This confirms Stone Stance's flat reduction is applied BEFORE damage is routed through wards, not after. As long as the incoming hit is ≤2 (Stone Stance's reduction) the ward is never touched, so overshoot sizing is only a real risk against hits >2.
5. R8 (T53), R10 (T76): 2HP/5HP wards fully absorbing Foreclosure(4) combined with active Stone Stance — 2HP ward eats 2, Stone Stance zeroes the other 2, net 0 damage taken, ward destroyed for exactly what it absorbed (no waste since 2HP=exactly what touched it).
6. R10 (T76): 5HP ward + Stone Stance(-2) vs Entropy(7) — 5+2=7, EXACT containment, zero damage taken, full 7 banked. This was the cleanest bumper-sizing play of the game.
7. R12 (T87): 4HP ward vs Saboteur's Kit(2) fired right at the round boundary before I could recast Stone Stance — full ward shattered for only 2 banked, 2 HP wasted. This is the one clear instance of the "sizing tax" biting: dooms that fire exactly at the new round's first tick can precede my first main-phase action, so Stone Stance's every-round upkeep has an unavoidable one-hit blind spot.

### Sizing-minigame verdict: real texture, with one caveat
It genuinely rewards planning — matching Fortify/Tithe output to known doom sizes (2/4/7) produced clean zero-damage turns repeatedly, and the Stone-Stance-absorbs-first interaction was a real discovery: it means small dooms (≤2) never need a ward at all, so the only sizing decisions that matter are for Foreclosure(4)-class and Entropy(7)-class hits. That's a tight, comprehensible puzzle, not busywork. The one sour note is Prophecy of Collapse: it's a flat "destroy largest ward" with no damage component, so it can NEVER be sized around — any ward I have becomes bait, and my Fortify-every-turn habit fed it for free five separate times (R2,R4,R6,R7,R8) for zero bank credit. Against a Collapse-heavy list the correct counter-play is to NOT keep a ward up when Collapse's fuse is live (let it fizzle on nothing) — I under-used this given components were often unavailable to react, but a more resource-flush pilot should simply stop Fortifying while Collapse is queued.

### Bot blind spots / rule notes
- Runic Seal cannot target a spell that's already been cast this round ('c'-flagged); it CAN target face-down/unrevealed prepared spells that haven't cast yet (confirmed via the sealPrepared choice list including FACEDOWN-n entries). Also confirmed: Runic Seal/Reckoning/Sealed Verdict all need the FULL component cost paid with matching symbol type (S vs V vs M pips) — a VS or SM card only supplies ONE S-pip, so SS costs need two separate S-bearing cards, not just two "symbol slots." This tripped me up several times and cost tempo.
- Sealed Verdict never fired all game — it's a Reaction that can only be cast in a live reaction window (opponent's spell on stack), not proactively on my own main turn; I kept trying to pre-attach it main-phase and getting no cast option, which is working as intended but is a genuine trap for a pilot who doesn't know the rule.
- The reshuffle-exhaustion penalty (2/4/6, per doctrine) hit both players once each (me R11, them R11) for 2 damage apiece — minor compared to the ledger swings, never a deciding factor here.
- Confirmed genuinely new interaction not in prior doctrine: Stone Stance's flat reduction is checked BEFORE ward routing, so it can fully absorb small dooms without ever touching/shattering a ward. This should probably be called out explicitly in future doctrine as it changes optimal ward sizing (only size bumpers for hits that exceed your active flat reduction).
