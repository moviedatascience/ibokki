## Analysis

RESULT: WIN for piloted Abjuration vs greedy Divination bot — 23 HP to -5, round 12 (seed 2103). Bot-level baseline for this matchup: 0-30 Div (avg 10.2 rounds). Human-grade piloting flipped it, and not narrowly: I finished at 23/30 HP having never dropped below 23, with 49 ward HP on board.

DAMAGE LEDGER
- Their total real damage on me over 12 rounds: 8 (Omen x2 landed = 4, Saboteur's Kit x3... one still pending at game end = 4 landed) + exhaustion 2 (my own reshuffle) — everything else (every Foretell all game) died in wards/reactions.
- My damage: Reckoning 8 + 8 + 10 (bank grew 16 -> ~18 -> ~21 prevented), Ward Pulse x6 = 6, total 32 in three rounds of actual offense after ten rounds of pure defense.

WHAT MATTERED (tool scorecard)
- Runic Seal: 4 casts, 4 relevant (Omen denied R1/R3/R4/R5-R8 pattern; R6 sealed Reclaim instead). THE early-game HP preserver — Omen uptime through R5: zero.
- Phase Shift (swapped in for Counterbind at L7): 2 Omens CANCELLED ON THE STACK (R7, R9), each burning their Seek-tutored MM fuel; plus a full round (R7) where the armed threat froze the bot into casting nothing. The load-bearing mid-game tool.
- Counterbind: prepped R5-R6, fired 0 times, swapped out. Its SM cost is wrong for Abj's S-flooded resource deck; Phase Shift (SS, cancels anything) strictly outperformed it in this matchup where every enemy spell is M-cost anyway.
- Wards (Fortify/Arcane Shell/Aegis Charm/Bulwark Shard/Sentinel Rune): peaked at 53 total HP. Every Foretell all game hit ward, not face — this IS the Reckoning bank. Wards don't stop dooms, but dooms weren't the bot's real damage; chip was, and chip fed my wincon.
- Grounding: prevent-1+draw-1, fired 3x — quietly excellent (bank + cards for S).
- Sentinel Rune (L2, VS): sneaky MVP late — converts dead VS components into a ward + 1 draw/round; kept the SS supply flowing for Reckonings.
- RECKONING: 8, 8, 10 across R10-R12. Repeatable each round at SS. This is the matchup's win condition, full stop. Final Reckoning (L4) never unlocked (L4 wasn't offered by level 12) and was never needed.

MECHANICAL FINDINGS (engine/balance-relevant)
1. TURN-ORDER DOOM EXPLOIT: in rounds where Div acts first, a doom cast as the round's first action is UNANSWERABLE — seal can't target an already-cast spell, and reaction fuel can't be pre-attached because attachments sweep at round end. Omens landed in exactly (and only) those windows: R6, R10, R11. In rounds where I act first, arm-Phase-Shift-turn-1 makes their doom strictly punished (cancelled + fuel burned). This asymmetry alternated and defined the whole midgame.
2. DETACH-RESCUE TECH: manually detaching unused reaction fuel back to hand on my last turn before round end dodges the sweep entirely. The sim bots never do this (they only detach-before-attach). This let me cycle one CMP-SS as a permanent Phase Shift threat/Reckoning warchest for four straight rounds. If this is unintended, sweep should probably also lock detach after cast slots are exhausted; if intended, bots should learn it.
3. Mana Drain auto-fired on the opponent's FIRST attach of the round (their Divine fuel) with no choice offered — near-worthless vs a component-rich opponent; I swapped it out R2. Possible SIMPLIFIED-pattern suspect: the reaction seems to fire on first trigger rather than held for a chosen attach.
4. Sealed Vault (their gambit) reset their 4-card deck to 38 with zero cost — it deleted the exhaustion wincon in one free play. Div ran its deck to 4 by R7 and never took a single exhaustion tick; I (the slow deck!) took exhaustion damage first (R9, -2). If exhaustion is meant to be Div's tax for draw-engine greed, Sealed Vault at 0 cost defeats the whole mechanism.
5. GREEDY BOT PASSIVITY: the bot never cast Cut the Thread in 12 rounds (even fueled, even with juicy targets), upgraded its L3 slots into three MMM draw spells instead of damage, and only swapped in Entropy at R12 when it was at 5 HP. It also repeatedly froze (casting nothing for whole rounds) when a fueled Phase Shift was showing. Its evaluation appears to underweight disruption and overvalue card economy it can't convert to damage. Abjuration's game plan barely had to interact with a real doom clock — Foreclosure/Entropy never threatened.
6. Reckoning bank sources confirmed: ward soak counts, reaction prevents count, and the bank persists/grows across the whole match — three casts totaling 26 damage off ~21 banked (the displayed hits 8/8/10 match floor(bank/2) as it grew).

VERDICT: Abj-vs-Div is decisively winnable at human level with the current cards — this wasn't a squeaker. The load-bearing tools, in order: Reckoning (wincon), Phase Shift (mid-game doom answer + fuel-burn), Runic Seal (early doom denial), ward stack (bank engine). Counterbind felt mispriced for its own school (SM in an S-flooded deck); Mana Drain felt broken-weak (uncontrollable trigger); Quenching Salts x2 were literal dead cards all game (no Burn source in the matchup); Sealed Vault feels mispriced at free. The bot's 0-30 record here reads as bot blindspots (never seals, never cancels, never detach-rescues, hoards draw) rather than an impossible matchup.
