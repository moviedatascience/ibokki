WARD ECONOMY vs THEIR CHIP: Fortify/Reflective Ward/Ward Pulse + free items (Bulwark Shard x2, Aegis Charm) kept 4-15 ward HP standing from R3 onward; wards ate every point that got past stance, including Fireball (5->3->soaked, ping back) and both Inferno Lances. Reflective Ward pings (3) + Overclock self-damage (2) completed their 30->17 slide — I dealt chip with defensive spells only.

THEIR REACTIONS' IMPACT: zero. Combust (punishes MY reactions for 4) was armed with VV in R8 and again R9/R10 — I simply never played a reaction (Absorb stayed dry all game; nothing threatened face through stance+wards), so Combust's fuel swept at round end twice. Searing Riposte never appeared. The bot spent ~5 V-cards arming a dead card.

WHERE THE BOT PUNISHED/SURPRISED ME: (1) Leading rounds 4/6 with Firebolt/Fireball BEFORE my stance was up — its best line; cost me ward HP but soaks charge Reckoning, so even its best line fed my wincon. (2) Dispelling Powder instantly killed a ward — fortunately my smallest (1HP); vs a fat-ward Ward Collapse plan this card is real tech, vs Reckoning it's a bank rounding error. SYSTEMATIC BOT GAPS OBSERVED: (a) never detaches unspent fuel before round end — swept 2-3 attached V-cards repeatedly (R3: VS+VS+VSM in one round) while I reclaimed mine every round; this alone is a multi-card-per-round economy gap vs a human. (b) arms Combust against an opponent with no fueled reactions — valuation should gate reaction-arming on opponent's reaction surface. (c) whole-round V droughts (R2 and R10: zero casts) — resource deck V-density vs its slot costs looks like the root cause; it ran its 40-card deck to 3 cards by R10 digging for V.

CARDS THAT FELT MISPRICED: Reckoning (SS for 20+ damage in any long game vs an aggro school) is drastically undercosted against spam — its cost scales with nothing while its payout scales with game length and opponent aggression; consider capping, pricing at SSS, or halving soak contribution. Ward Pulse at S is excellent but fair. Stonewarden and Absorb were dead prep slots in practice (stance+wards already blanked everything — a leaner list wins the same way). Quenching Salts/Second Wind never needed.

VERDICT: Skilled Abjuration crushes greedy Evocation — this pilot never faced a lethal threat after round 1 and won with 93% HP intact. The 53% bot number reads as a bot-skill artifact, not a balance signal: the greedy Evo bot's damage output is real, but greedy Abj clearly fails to (1) stance first-seat every round, (2) detach-reclaim fuel, (3) hold Reckoning until lethal, and (4) starve enemy reactions by not reacting. Piloted record on 'bot-unwinnable' edges is now 8-0. If Evo is meant to be competitive INTO Abj at high skill, it needs either ward pierce in tier 1-2 or Reckoning needs a nerf; on current cards, design intent (Abj > Evo) is over-achieved at skill.
