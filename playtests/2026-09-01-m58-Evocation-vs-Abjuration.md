## Analysis

**Result + margin + round:** Dominant Abjuration win, round 10, essentially a one-sided race — P1 never dropped below 27 HP the entire game (started 30, took exactly 3 unmitigated points total in R1-R2 before the ward economy came fully online), while P0 (greedy Evo) was bled down by chip damage from Ward Pulse and then killed outright by a single Reckoning cast for 28.

**Decisive lines:**
1. Stone Stance cast round 1 and recast every round (sometimes late in the round due to the one-cast-per-turn cadence) shaved 2 off every Evo spell before ward routing — turned buffed Fireball (6) into 4, buffed Inferno Lance (5) into 3, Firebolt (3) into 1, Hex Bolt (3) into 1. Under Stone Stance the +1 bumps landed as if unbuffed (Fireball played like the old 5, Lance like the old 4) — the buff was almost entirely absorbed by Stone Stance rather than by ward sizing.
2. Fortify every idle cast slot rebuilt/grew a single ward round over round (2→4→...→9 HP at peak), and Ward Pulse (cast whenever a slot was free and a ward was up) both grew the ward +1 and chipped 1 face damage per cast — over the game this chip alone took P0 from 30 to 22 before Reckoning even fired.
3. The killing blow: Reckoning (ABJ-032, unlocked R10) reads "half the total damage prevented this match" and — critically — does NOT spend/consume that pool the way Warding Tithe/Sealed Verdict/Restoring Rune explicitly do. By R10 the ward-soak bank had reached 56 (every Fireball/Lance/Hex Bolt/Wrath of the Mage that hit a ward fed it, plus the earlier ward that Dispelling Powder destroyed outright still apparently registered as prevented). 56/2=28 one-shot the bot from 22 HP.
4. Never needed to spend a single reaction (Absorb, Sealed Verdict never fired) — the bot's spell selection was too weak post-Stone-Stance to ever threaten face damage large enough to justify burning a reaction; wards alone handled everything, banking the full damage each time.

**HYPOTHESIS VERDICT:** Piloted Abjuration found its edge comfortably, arguably MORE comfortably than the pre-buff baseline — the +1 bumps to Fireball/Lance were fully neutralized by Stone Stance's flat -2, so the "buff" never actually changed the ward-sizing math in practice (a Stone-Stance'd buffed Fireball = an un-buffed pre-patch Fireball).
